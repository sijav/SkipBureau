The rule case is the tax number and the foreigner identity number in Turkey: the numbers that make you exist to the state.

This is a small case and I want it closed quickly and exactly. What I have already, which you need not re-derive: the foreigner identity number begins with 99 and exists once the person is in the foreigner records; a potential tax number is obtained when a tax-linked transaction needs one; both are available centrally; and no official nationwide "obtain within X days of arrival" deadline was found.

Five short questions.

1. ARE THEY THE SAME NUMBER OR TWO NUMBERS? I have read both. For a foreigner who holds a residence permit, is the 99 number used as their tax number, or do they hold a separate potential tax number as well? If the 99 number serves as the tax number, say when that became true and what happens to a potential tax number somebody obtained before they had a residence permit.

2. WHO CAN GET A TAX NUMBER, AND FROM WHERE. Can a person with no residence permit, newly arrived on a visa, obtain one? Online, in person, or both? Is a passport enough? Can somebody outside Turkey obtain one, and does that route differ?

3. WHAT IT IS ACTUALLY NEEDED FOR, in the first month of a person's life in Turkey. Opening a bank account, signing a lease, getting a mobile line, paying a residence permit fee, buying property. For each, say whether the number is legally required or merely usual, because a guide that says "you need a tax number for X" when the law says nothing sends people on an errand.

4. THE COST AND THE TIME. Is there a fee? How long does it take, and is it issued immediately at the counter or online?

5. WHAT COULD GO WRONG. Is there any circumstance in which a person ends up with two tax numbers, or with a number that does not match their identity documents, and what is the official remedy? I ask because a mismatched number is the kind of thing that surfaces months later at a bank and nobody writes about it.

Answer only these five, and keep it short where the answer is short.

## Answered

1. **Two possible identifiers, but the 99 number serves as the tax number once assigned.** Since **1 July 2010**, foreigners who have a foreigner identity number must use it for transactions requiring a tax identifier. This is nationwide: **no regional variation in this rule**. [MKK’s official notice](https://www.mkk.com.tr/tr-tr/Genel-Mektuplar/Sayfalar/Yabanci-Kimlik-Numarasi-Bildirimi.aspx) expressly confirms this.

   A previously obtained potential tax number does not remain an alternative number to present for those transactions. **I could not verify from an accessible official source exactly how the earlier tax record is linked, whether that happens automatically, or what application the holder must make.** I would not publish “automatically merged” or “automatically cancelled.”

2. **A residence permit is not a prerequisite.** A newly arrived foreigner without a foreigner identity number can obtain a potential tax number. Both online and counter routes are documented. For the counter route, bring the **original passport, a copy of its identification page, and a signed application**; the application includes address information. “Passport alone” therefore understates the paperwork. [Public-university instructions](https://www.uludag.edu.tr/en/uluyos/tax-identification-number-information-69202), [GİB application](https://dijital.gib.gov.tr/foreigners/kimlikNoBasvuru).

   **Office procedure:** Istanbul’s November 2020 notice directs applicants online and limits counter applications to identity/passport-verification failures. Its continuing enforcement today is unverified; this is not a different regional eligibility law. [Istanbul Migration notice](https://istanbul.goc.gov.tr/yabancilarin-vergi-kimlik-numarasi-temini-hakkinda).

   **Outside Turkey:** GİB documents a route for nonresident investors through banks, brokers or custodians submitting the application and supporting documents. **I could not verify a general pre-arrival self-service online entitlement**, including whether someone who has never entered Turkey can complete the ordinary online form. [GİB guide, section 25](https://cdn.gib.gov.tr/api/gibportal-file/file/getFileResources?objectKey=arsiv/fileadmin/beyannamerehberi/2025/2025_GVK_Gecici.pdf).

3. **The five transactions:**

   | Transaction | What is verified |
   |---|---|
   | Opening a bank account | A tax identifier is a **legal requirement**, not merely bank custom; the 99 number fulfils that role when available. Türkiye’s official country note identifies banking transactions as legislatively covered. |
   | Signing an ordinary residential lease | **I could not verify a general legal requirement** to obtain a tax number merely to sign it. Notarization and subsequent administrative uses need separate analysis. |
   | Getting a mobile line | **I could not verify a tax-number requirement.** An official 2021 decision quotes telecom rules accepting foreigners’ passports or equivalent identity documents, but I have not verified the complete current rules. Do not publish a mandatory tax-number errand on this evidence. |
   | Paying residence-permit fees | **A separate tax-number application is not established as universally necessary.** Migration Management expressly permits payment using the residence application number. This is an official payment procedure. |
   | Buying property | A tax identifier is **legally required for the land-registry transaction**, according to Türkiye’s official country note. This does not establish a need for a separate potential tax number when a 99 number exists. |

   Sources: [Türkiye’s TIN country note](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/topics/policy-issue-focus/aeoi/turkiye-tin.pdf), [KVKK decision](https://www.kvkk.gov.tr/Icerik/7135/2021-859), [Migration Management FAQ](https://www.goc.gov.tr/ikamet-sss). These are national rules/procedures; **no regional legal variation was verified**.

4. **Cost: free**, explicitly stated by Bursa Uludağ University, a public university. **Online:** its instructions say the system supplies the number after submission; Istanbul Migration likewise describes the number appearing on the resulting page. **Counter:** I could not verify a current nationwide processing time or guarantee of immediate issuance. [University instructions](https://www.uludag.edu.tr/en/uluyos/tax-identification-number-information-69202).

5. **The verified failure is identity/passport verification failing even when the applicant entered the information correctly.** The published remedy is a tax-office application. [Istanbul Migration notice](https://istanbul.goc.gov.tr/yabancilarin-vergi-kimlik-numarasi-temini-hakkinda).

   **I could not verify an official account of duplicate potential tax numbers, or a published procedure for correcting an already-issued number’s identity details or merging duplicates.** The earlier potential number followed by a 99 number is distinguishable from two erroneously issued potential numbers. The latter, and its remedy, remain open.

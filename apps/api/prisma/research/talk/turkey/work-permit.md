## Answered

1. **Who can apply without an employer**

These routes are national: **no regional variation in the rules described here.** I did not verify any differing provincial office practice.

| Person | Can they apply for themselves? |
|---|---|
| **Freelancer** | **Yes, through an independent work permit.** This is discretionary: the Ministry considers qualifications, experience and economic/employment contribution. Being a freelancer does not itself establish eligibility or permission to work. |
| **Owner wanting to work in their Turkish company** | **An owner/business-partner route exists.** The Ministry says to establish/register the business first, then apply before starting work. It expressly allows owners/partners to receive a fixed-term permit; an independent permit is not invariably required. **I could not verify the precise current e-İzin self-filing procedure**, so I would not yet publish “log in as an individual and apply.” |
| **Spouse of a Turkish citizen** | **Marriage alone does not establish an employer-free work entitlement.** Law 6735, Article 16(1)(g), allows exceptional treatment for someone married to a Turkish citizen and living together in Türkiye. It does not itself establish an open work permit. I could not verify a separate self-application route based solely on marriage. Employment still needs the applicable employment route; self-employment needs its applicable route. |
| **Long-term residence holder** | **Yes, they may apply for a permanent work permit without an employer.** Approval is not automatic. The Ministry describes this permit as independent of an employer; long-term residence itself should not be presented as the work permit. |

Sources: [Ministry permit types](https://csgb.gov.tr/uigm/en/calisma-izni/calisma-izni-turleri/), [Ministry business-owner guidance](https://www.csgb.gov.tr/uigm/en/general-information/conditions-and-process-for-foreigners-to-open-a-company-workplace-in-turkiye/), and [Law 6735, Article 16](https://cdn.tbmm.gov.tr/KKBSPublicFile/D26/Y1/T1/KanunMetni/5d405954-cd2b-452e-bc31-1d716ad257a3.html). The Investment Office’s [legal guide](https://agencyadm.invest.gov.tr/en/library/publications/lists/investpublications/legal-guide-to-investing-in-turkiye.pdf) expressly identifies personal applications for independent and permanent permits, but **I could retrieve only its indexed official extract; opening the complete PDF failed.**

2. **The six months**

**It is not six months remaining.** The Ministry’s current FAQ says the residence permit must have been **issued for at least six months and remain valid on the work-permit application date**. Its application leaflet explicitly says six months “in total.” This is the published national administrative requirement. [Ministry FAQ](https://www.csgb.gov.tr/en/frequently-asked-questions/directorate-general-of-international-labour-force/work-permit/), [application leaflet, page 2](https://www.csgb.gov.tr/Media/vcsf22vp/ad%C4%B1m-ad%C4%B1m-%C3%A7al%C4%B1%C5%9Fma-izni-ba%C5%9Fvurusu-eng.pdf).

Consequently, **a qualifying six- or twelve-month residence permit with five months left satisfies this particular duration condition**. That conclusion follows from the published wording. There is no basis here for telling that person to extend first or apply abroad merely because five months remain.

I did **not** verify an actual refusal or redirection practice for that situation. Nor did I verify what happens if the residence permit expires while the work-permit application is pending. The underlying regulation’s official links failed to load, so that latter issue remains unresolved.

3. **Employer requirements in 2026**

The Ministry publishes these as **national assessment criteria**, not provincial office practices:

- **Balance-sheet businesses:** five Turkish employees per foreigner.
- **New businesses:** ₺500,000 paid-in capital.
- **Established businesses:** ₺500,000 paid-in capital **or** ₺8 million net sales **or** US$150,000 exports.

Salary uses the gross minimum wage **at application**. The official 2026 monthly base is **₺33,030**; the calculated thresholds are:

| Job | Multiple | Gross monthly salary |
|---|---:|---:|
| Senior executives/pilots | 5× | ₺165,150 |
| Engineers/architects | 4× | ₺132,120 |
| Other managers | 3× | ₺99,090 |
| Expertise/mastery jobs | 2× | ₺66,060 |
| Domestic/other jobs | 1× | ₺33,030 |

Main exceptions:

- ₺50m annual net sales: quota waived for five foreigners.
- Three years’ lawful residence within five, excluding study: quota/financial relief for three; foreigners cannot outnumber Turks.
- Long-term residents and spouses married to Turks ≥three years: quota/financial/salary relief.
- IT, R&D, education, public projects, tourism and livestock: conditional sector relief.
- Company partners: ₺500,000 capital share, ≥20% ownership; five Turks from month seven. US$100,000 share waives those tests.

Sources: [current assessment criteria, including 2026 amendments](https://www.csgb.gov.tr/uigm/en/calisma-izni/calisma-izni-degerlendirme-kriterleri/), [official 2026 minimum wage](https://www.csgb.gov.tr/tr/poco-pages/asgari-ucret/).

These exceptions are not exhaustive. **Passing the financial and staffing tests does not establish that the occupation is permitted.** Health and education work can require prior authorisation; reserved professions remain a separate check. [Ministry application leaflet](https://www.csgb.gov.tr/Media/vcsf22vp/ad%C4%B1m-ad%C4%B1m-%C3%A7al%C4%B1%C5%9Fma-izni-ba%C5%9Fvurusu-eng.pdf), [reserved professions](https://www.csgb.gov.tr/uigm/calisma-izni/turk-vatandaslarina-hasredilen-meslekler/).

4. **Leaving the employer or being dismissed**

**The permit does not transfer automatically.** A different employer requires a new permit; Article 10(2) treats that application under the initial-permit rules. The existing permit does not authorise work for the new employer. [Law 6735](https://cdn.tbmm.gov.tr/KKBSPublicFile/D26/Y1/T1/KanunMetni/5d405954-cd2b-452e-bc31-1d716ad257a3.html), [Ministry FAQ](https://www.csgb.gov.tr/en/frequently-asked-questions/directorate-general-of-international-labour-force/work-permit/).

**Legal rule:** Article 15 provides for cancellation when employment ends for any reason, and says validity ends through expiry or Ministry cancellation. Article 22 requires the employer to notify the Ministry within **15 days**. That reporting deadline does **not** establish a 15-day grace period. [Law 6735, Articles 15 and 22](https://cdn.tbmm.gov.tr/KKBSPublicFile/D26/Y1/T1/KanunMetni/5d405954-cd2b-452e-bc31-1d716ad257a3.html).

**Published administration:** the Ministry’s domestic-employment guide shows a termination-date field, resignation/dismissal reasons, and the 15-day reporting requirement. This is national system guidance, not evidence of a particular office’s behaviour. [Guide, page 21](https://www.csgb.gov.tr/Media/goecl1vk/ev-hizmetlerinde-istihdam-edilecek-yabancilarin-%C3%A7ali%C5%9Fma-izni-ba%C5%9Fvuru-s%C3%BCre%C3%A7leri.pdf).

**I could not verify the precise effective cancellation date or a protected job-search/residence grace period.** I would publish neither “it ends automatically that instant” nor “you safely have X days to find another employer.”

An additional restriction matters: certain sector-exempt permits, and overseas domestic-work/livestock permits, face adverse assessment for employer changes within their first six months, except force majeure. [Assessment criteria, B.3.5, B.10.4 and B.12.1](https://www.csgb.gov.tr/uigm/en/calisma-izni/calisma-izni-degerlendirme-kriterleri/).

5. **Professions reserved to Turkish citizens**

**Yes—an official consolidated list exists.** The Ministry’s **“Türk Vatandaşlarına Hasredilen Meslekler”** page lists occupations and cites the corresponding laws or regulations and articles. Examples include dentistry, pharmacy, veterinary medicine, advocacy, notarial work, private security and tourist guiding. These are national legal restrictions: **no provincial variation**. [Official consolidated list](https://www.csgb.gov.tr/uigm/calisma-izni/turk-vatandaslarina-hasredilen-meslekler/).

However, I verified **the existence and contents of the compilation**, not the current validity and every exception of each underlying provision. The Ministry’s English FAQ describes its own list as the best-known examples. I would publish: **“Check the Ministry’s consolidated list, then confirm the provision and any exception applicable to your profession.”** I would not publish “there is no official list,” or treat absence from it as proof that a profession is open.

- **A residence application route exists.** Article **21(6) of the Regulation implementing Law 6458** provides for residence applications made within ten days by people whose work permits were cancelled or ended, assessed under Article 22 of Law 6458. I verified this through the Interior Ministry’s **Provincial Migration Directorate Inspection Guide**, section **04.04.06, printed page 10**, which explicitly checks compliance with that provision. This is a national regulatory rule, not an office concession. [Interior Ministry inspection guide](https://www.icisleri.gov.tr/kurumlar/icisleri.gov.tr/IcSite/mulkiyeteftis/Teftis-Rehberleri/IL-GOC-IDARESI-MUDURLUGU-TEFTIS-REHBERI.pdf).

I did **not** verify equally explicit notification wording for cancellation following termination of employment. I would not transfer the extension-refusal rule to cancellation without confirmation. [Same guide, section 04.04.07](https://www.icisleri.gov.tr/kurumlar/icisleri.gov.tr/IcSite/mulkiyeteftis/Teftis-Rehberleri/IL-GOC-IDARESI-MUDURLUGU-TEFTIS-REHBERI.pdf).

**What remains unverified:** whether the interval before that residence application is itself classified as lawful residence, and the precise cancellation-versus-notification date used to calculate its deadline. The ten-day application provision does not establish permission to work or an unconditional job-search period.

There is also a separate **15–30-day departure period following a deportation decision**, subject to exceptions, under Article 56. That is not an automatic period granted when someone loses their job. [Law 6458, Article 56](https://www.goc.gov.tr/kurumlar/goc.gov.tr/Kanunlar/YUKK/YUKK-TURKCE.pdf).

2. **Residence expiring while the initial work-permit application is pending**

It provides an application document with which the applicant may remain until a decision, even after the existing residence permit expires. This protection attaches to the **residence-extension application**. [Law 6458, Article 24(2)](https://www.goc.gov.tr/kurumlar/goc.gov.tr/Kanunlar/YUKK/YUKK-TURKCE.pdf).

[Ministry FAQ](https://www.csgb.gov.tr/en/frequently-asked-questions/directorate-general-of-international-labour-force/work-permit/).

## What a reader is told

**Who applies.** Normally your employer applies for you, electronically. For the
ordinary in-country route, the application is made against a residence permit
that was **issued for at least six months and is still valid on the day of the
application**. Note what that does not say: it is not six months *remaining*. A
one-year permit with five months left still satisfies it.

**Whether your job can produce a permit at all.** The Ministry publishes
national criteria, and they are about your employer as much as you. A business
keeping a balance sheet needs **five Turkish employees for each foreigner**. A
new business needs **500,000 lira** of paid-in capital; an established one needs
that, or **8 million lira** of net sales, or **150,000 US dollars** of exports.

Your salary must be **at least the applicable multiple** of the gross minimum
wage in force on the day you apply: five times for senior executives and pilots,
four for engineers and architects, three for other managers, twice for jobs
requiring expertise or mastery, and once for domestic and other work. The gross
minimum wage for 2026 is **33,030 lira** a month (net 28,075.50). We give you
the multipliers and the wage separately because the Ministry publishes them
separately; we did not verify an official page publishing the multiplied
figures.

**The exemptions are worth checking before you give up.** Fifty million lira of
annual net sales waives the employee quota for five foreigners. Since **3 August
2026**, domestic applications can receive employment and financial-criteria
relief for up to three foreigners per workplace who spent at least **one year
within the preceding three** lawfully in Turkey under a work permit, residence
permit or international protection; foreign permit-holders must not outnumber
Turkish employees. Long-term residence permit holders and foreigners living in a
marital union with a Turkish citizen for at least three years are **exempt from
the employment, financial and salary criteria**. There is conditional relief in
IT, research, education, public projects, tourism and livestock.

For balance-sheet businesses, an applicant **partner** needs at least a
500,000-lira capital share and twenty per cent ownership, and the business needs
at least 500,000 lira paid-in capital; five Turkish employees are required
monthly from month seven of the initial permit. A capital share of at least
**US$100,000** waives these tests.

**Passing those tests is not the same as being allowed to do the job.** Some
professions are reserved to Turkish citizens. The Ministry publishes a
consolidated list with the law behind each entry, covering dentistry, pharmacy,
veterinary medicine, advocacy, notarial work, private security and tourist
guiding among others. Check that list, then confirm the provision and any
exception that applies to your own profession. Health and education work can
need separate authorisation first.

**If you leave the job or lose it.** Your permit does not move with you. Working
for a different employer needs a new permit, and the law treats that application
as a first application rather than a transfer. When the employment ends the
permit is liable to cancellation, and your employer must tell the Ministry
within **fifteen days**. That fifteen days is the **employer's reporting duty**
and it is not a grace period for you.

There is a separate **ten-day window for a residence-permit application**:
Article 21(6) of the regulation implementing Law 6458 lets someone whose work
permit has been cancelled or has ended apply for a residence permit within ten
days. The residence right that came from the work permit ends with it; a
separate, still-valid residence permit is not invalidated by that cancellation.
We could not verify whether the days before that application count as lawful
residence, or exactly which date the ten days run from, and the provision gives
neither permission to work nor an unconditional period to look for a job.

**Do not assume a pending application protects you.** If your residence permit
expires while a **first** work permit application is being decided, we could not
find a rule that extends your stay. The Ministry permits continued work while a
timely **extension** application is assessed, for no more than ninety days and
with the same work and workplace; this does not cover a first application. The
Presidency says plainly that residence permits are not issued for the purpose of
applying for a work permit.

**Fees.** The standard 2026 fee for a fixed-term work permit of up to one year
is **12,574.90 lira**, plus **964 lira** for the card. The tariff distinguishes
independent, permanent and temporary-protection permits.

# Working in Turkey

**Agreed 2026-09-12**, after three turns in `talk/turkey/work-permit.md`. Every
sentence below is the corrected one, and none of it may be softened, sharpened
or given colour when it becomes a guide.

- It **corrected its own answer from two turns earlier** when asked to check
  this wording, having gone back to the Turkish-language criteria page and
  found a change effective **3 August 2026**[^csgb-criteria-a-4]. A conversation that never
  re-reads itself would have published the old rule.
- Asked whether a salary table was official or its own arithmetic, it said

## What a reader is told

**Who applies.** Normally your employer applies for you, electronically. For the
ordinary in-country route, the application is made against a residence permit
that was **issued for at least six months and is still valid on the day of the
application**[^csgb-faq-9-six-months]. Note what that does not say: it is not six months *remaining*. A
one-year permit with five months left still satisfies it.

**Whether your job can produce a permit at all.** The Ministry publishes
national criteria, and they are about your employer as much as you. A business
keeping a balance sheet needs **five Turkish employees for each foreigner**[^csgb-criteria-a-1-1]. A
new business needs **500,000 lira**[^csgb-criteria-a-2] of paid-in capital; an established one needs
that, or **8 million lira**[^csgb-criteria-a-2] of net sales, or **150,000 US dollars**[^csgb-criteria-a-2] of exports.

Your salary must be **at least the applicable multiple**[^csgb-criteria-a-3-1] of the gross minimum
wage in force on the day you apply: five times for senior executives and pilots,
four for engineers and architects, three for other managers, twice for jobs
requiring expertise or mastery, and once for domestic and other work[^csgb-criteria-a-3-1]. The gross
minimum wage for 2026 is **33,030 lira** a month[^csgb-minimum-wage-2026] (net 28,075.50[^csgb-minimum-wage-2026-net]). We give you
the multipliers and the wage separately because the Ministry publishes them
separately; we did not verify an official page publishing the multiplied
figures.

**The exemptions are worth checking before you give up.** Fifty million lira of
annual net sales[^csgb-criteria-a-1-2] waives the employee quota for five foreigners[^csgb-criteria-a-1-2]. Since **3 August
2026**[^csgb-criteria-a-4], domestic applications can receive employment and financial-criteria
relief for up to three foreigners[^csgb-criteria-a-4] per workplace who spent at least **one year
within the preceding three**[^csgb-criteria-a-4] lawfully in Turkey under a work permit, residence
permit or international protection; foreign permit-holders must not outnumber
Turkish employees[^csgb-criteria-a-4]. Long-term residence permit holders and foreigners living in a
marital union with a Turkish citizen for at least three years[^csgb-criteria-cc-1-1-e] are **exempt from
the employment, financial and salary criteria**. There is conditional relief in
IT, research, education, public projects, tourism and livestock.

For balance-sheet businesses, an applicant **partner** needs at least a
500,000-lira capital share[^csgb-criteria-c-1-1-a] and twenty per cent ownership[^csgb-criteria-c-1-1-a], and the business needs
at least 500,000 lira paid-in capital[^csgb-criteria-c-1-1-a]; five Turkish employees are required
monthly from month seven of the initial permit[^csgb-criteria-c-1-1-b]. A capital share of at least
**US$100,000**[^csgb-criteria-c-1-2] waives these tests.

**Passing those tests is not the same as being allowed to do the job.** Some
professions are reserved to Turkish citizens. The Ministry publishes a
consolidated list with the law behind each entry, covering dentistry, pharmacy,
veterinary medicine, advocacy, notarial work, private security and tourist
guiding among others. Check that list, then confirm the provision and any
exception that applies to your own profession. Health and education work can
need separate authorisation first.

**If you leave the job or lose it.** Your permit does not move with you. Working
for a different employer needs a new permit, and the law treats that application
as a first application rather than a transfer. When the employment ends the
permit is liable to cancellation, and your employer must tell the Ministry
within **fifteen days**[^law6735-22-1-fifteen-days][^csgb-faq-59-fifteen-days]. That fifteen days is the **employer's reporting duty**[^law6735-22-1-fifteen-days]
and it is not a grace period for you.

There is a separate **ten-day window for a residence-permit application**[^yukk-reg-21-6-ten-days]:
Article 21(6) of the regulation implementing Law 6458 lets someone whose work
permit has been cancelled or has ended apply for a residence permit within ten
days[^yukk-reg-21-6-ten-days]. The residence right that came from the work permit ends with it; a
separate, still-valid residence permit is not invalidated by that cancellation.
We could not verify whether the days before that application count as lawful
residence, or exactly which date the ten days run from, and the provision gives
neither permission to work nor an unconditional period to look for a job.

**Do not assume a pending application protects you.** If your residence permit
expires while a **first** work permit application is being decided, we could not
find a rule that extends your stay. The Ministry permits continued work while a
timely **extension** application is assessed, for no more than ninety days[^ilf-reg-27-5-ninety-days][^csgb-faq-21-ninety-days] and
with the same work and workplace; this does not cover a first application. The
Presidency says plainly that residence permits are not issued for the purpose of
applying for a work permit.

**Fees.** The standard 2026 fee for a fixed-term work permit of up to one year
is **12,574.90 lira**[^csgb-fee-page-12574][^law492-tariff-iv-12574], plus **964 lira**[^csgb-fee-page-card-964][^hmb-97-work-permit-card-964] for the card. The tariff distinguishes
independent, permanent and temporary-protection permits.

# Working in Turkey

sentence below is the corrected one, and none of it may be softened, sharpened
or given colour when it becomes a guide.

  this wording, having gone back to the Turkish-language criteria page and

## What a reader is told

**Who applies.** Normally your employer applies for you, electronically. For the
ordinary in-country route, the application is made against a residence permit
that was **issued for at least six months and is still valid on the day of the
application**[^csgb-faq-9-six-months]. Note what that does not say: it is not six months *remaining*. A
one-year permit with five months left still satisfies it.

**Whether your job can produce a permit at all.** The Ministry publishes
national criteria, and they are about your employer as much as you. A business
keeping a balance sheet needs **five Turkish employees for each foreigner**[^csgb-criteria-a-1-1]. A
new business needs **500,000 lira**[^csgb-criteria-a-2] of paid-in capital; an established one needs
that, or **8 million lira**[^csgb-criteria-a-2] of net sales, or **150,000 US dollars**[^csgb-criteria-a-2] of exports.

Your salary must be **at least the applicable multiple**[^csgb-criteria-a-3-1] of the gross minimum
wage in force on the day you apply: five times for senior executives and pilots,
four for engineers and architects, three for other managers, twice for jobs
requiring expertise or mastery, and once for domestic and other work[^csgb-criteria-a-3-1]. The gross
minimum wage for 2026 is **33,030 lira** a month[^csgb-minimum-wage-2026] (net 28,075.50[^csgb-minimum-wage-2026-net]). We give you
the multipliers and the wage separately because the Ministry publishes them
separately; we did not verify an official page publishing the multiplied
figures.

**The exemptions are worth checking before you give up.** Fifty million lira of
annual net sales[^csgb-criteria-a-1-2] waives the employee quota for five foreigners[^csgb-criteria-a-1-2]. Since **3 August
2026**[^csgb-criteria-a-4], domestic applications can receive employment and financial-criteria
relief for up to three foreigners[^csgb-criteria-a-4] per workplace who spent at least **one year
within the preceding three**[^csgb-criteria-a-4] lawfully in Turkey under a work permit, residence
permit or international protection; foreign permit-holders must not outnumber
Turkish employees[^csgb-criteria-a-4]. Long-term residence permit holders and foreigners living in a
marital union with a Turkish citizen for at least three years[^csgb-criteria-cc-1-1-e] are **exempt from
the employment, financial and salary criteria**. There is conditional relief in
IT, research, education, public projects, tourism and livestock.

For balance-sheet businesses, an applicant **partner** needs at least a
500,000-lira capital share[^csgb-criteria-c-1-1-a] and twenty per cent ownership[^csgb-criteria-c-1-1-a], and the business needs
at least 500,000 lira paid-in capital[^csgb-criteria-c-1-1-a]; five Turkish employees are required
monthly from month seven of the initial permit[^csgb-criteria-c-1-1-b]. A capital share of at least
**US$100,000**[^csgb-criteria-c-1-2] waives these tests.

**Passing those tests is not the same as being allowed to do the job.** Some
professions are reserved to Turkish citizens. The Ministry publishes a
consolidated list with the law behind each entry, covering dentistry, pharmacy,
veterinary medicine, advocacy, notarial work, private security and tourist
guiding among others. Check that list, then confirm the provision and any
exception that applies to your own profession. Health and education work can
need separate authorisation first.

**If you leave the job or lose it.** Your permit does not move with you. Working
for a different employer needs a new permit, and the law treats that application
as a first application rather than a transfer. When the employment ends the
permit is liable to cancellation, and your employer must tell the Ministry
within **fifteen days**[^law6735-22-1-fifteen-days][^csgb-faq-59-fifteen-days]. That fifteen days is the **employer's reporting duty**[^law6735-22-1-fifteen-days]
and it is not a grace period for you.

There is a separate **ten-day window for a residence-permit application**[^yukk-reg-21-6-ten-days]:
Article 21(6) of the regulation implementing Law 6458 lets someone whose work
permit has been cancelled or has ended apply for a residence permit within ten
days[^yukk-reg-21-6-ten-days]. The residence right that came from the work permit ends with it; a
separate, still-valid residence permit is not invalidated by that cancellation.
We could not verify whether the days before that application count as lawful
residence, or exactly which date the ten days run from, and the provision gives
neither permission to work nor an unconditional period to look for a job.

**Do not assume a pending application protects you.** If your residence permit
expires while a **first** work permit application is being decided, we could not
find a rule that extends your stay. The Ministry permits continued work while a
timely **extension** application is assessed, for no more than ninety days[^ilf-reg-27-5-ninety-days][^csgb-faq-21-ninety-days] and
with the same work and workplace; this does not cover a first application. The
Presidency says plainly that residence permits are not issued for the purpose of
applying for a work permit.

**Fees.** The standard 2026 fee for a fixed-term work permit of up to one year
is **12,574.90 lira**[^csgb-fee-page-12574][^law492-tariff-iv-12574], plus **964 lira**[^csgb-fee-page-card-964][^hmb-97-work-permit-card-964] for the card. The tariff distinguishes
independent, permanent and temporary-protection permits.

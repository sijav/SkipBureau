   **“A 2014 Revenue Administration letter specifies conversion at the central bank effective selling rate on the permit document’s issue date. We have not verified how today’s e-Ikamet assessment implements that instruction.”** [Official correspondence](https://pdb.ibu.edu.tr/Files/ckFiles/pdb-test-ibu-edu-tr/Mevzuat/Y%C3%96K%20Kararlar%C4%B1/yabanc%C4%B1uyruklu/10072014yabanciuyrukluogretimelemanicalismaizni.pdf).

5. **“The image itself carries no publication date.”**

   That absence was not verified. Replace with:  
   **“We have not established the image’s publication date.”**

6. **“Any one of these: … or a private policy.”**

   This admits documents and policies without the required qualifications. Replace with:  
   **“Any one of these: the prescribed signed/e-signed and stamped/sealed provincial social-security certificate confirming healthcare entitlement under a bilateral agreement, SGK provision document, or SGK general-health-insurance application certificate; or a private policy meeting the official minimum coverage requirements.”** Those insurance standards are national, with no regional variation. [Checklist](https://e-ikamet.goc.gov.tr/Ikamet/BasvuruIstenenBelgeler/BasvuruFormuIstenenBelgeler?tur=0), [SEDDK standard](https://seddk.gov.tr/upload/Bas%C4%B1n%20duyurusu.27-12-24.pdf).

7. **“Applicants under eighteen and over sixty-five do not have to provide it.”**

   The older official guidance requires submission of existing valid insurance even for these ages. Replace with:  
   **“Applicants under eighteen and over sixty-five are exempt from obtaining health insurance for the application; official guidance says existing valid cover must still be submitted.”** [Source](https://e-ikamet.goc.gov.tr/Ikamet/IstenenBelgeler/BasvuruFormuBelgelerIliskinAciklamalar).

8. **“A lease in your own name is not required.”**

   This can imply that somebody else’s lease alone suffices. Replace with:  
   **“A lease is not the only accepted way to prove your accommodation.”** [Checklist](https://e-ikamet.goc.gov.tr/Ikamet/BasvuruIstenenBelgeler/BasvuruFormuIstenenBelgeler?tur=0).

9. **The address-checklist sentence:** two fragments are too broad.

   Replace **“a recent utility bill or new subscription”** with **“a recent electricity, water, gas or landline-telephone bill, or the corresponding subscription agreement for a new subscriber.”**

   Replace **“a signed and stamped document from a student dormitory”** with **“a signed/e-signed and stamped/sealed dormitory document confirming your stay.”** [Checklist](https://e-ikamet.goc.gov.tr/Ikamet/BasvuruIstenenBelgeler/BasvuruFormuIstenenBelgeler?tur=0).

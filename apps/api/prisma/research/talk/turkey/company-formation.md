## Answered

1. **Formation costs in 2026**

There is **no single national all-in formation price**. Capital, the statutory registration-fee exemption and the competition levy are national; chamber registration and service charges vary.

| Item | Verified amount or rule |
|---|---|
| **Minimum capital** | **₺50,000.** For cash capital, **no statutory pre-registration payment** is required. The full subscribed amount must be paid within **24 months after registration**, subject to an earlier payment schedule in the articles or set by the managers. Capital is company funding, not a government fee. **No regional variation.** |
| **Statutory trade-registry formation fee — harç** | **₺0: formation is exempt.** This does **not** exempt the company from registry service charges or publication costs. **No regional variation in the exemption.** |
| **Competition Authority levy** | **0.04% of subscribed company capital**, collected through the registry at formation. At ₺50,000 capital: **₺20**. **No regional variation.** |
| **Trade Registry Gazette publication** | The 2026 tariff charges **₺2.48 per word** for company-formation announcements. The word count determines the bill. |
| **Istanbul registry services** | Its 2026 formation formula lists **₺2,160 formation certification + ₺2,760 opening-book certification + publication + ₺250 publication expenses**. An in-person signature declaration is separately listed at **₺1,720**; I have not verified when that charge is additional in an individual formation file. |
| **Chamber registration** | Istanbul’s new-capital-company category is **₺3,305**. Antalya’s 2026 new-registration category is **₺4,875**. These are **local tariffs**, not different company-capital laws. |
| **Notary and translation** | There is **no verified flat “foreigner formation notary fee.”** It depends on documents, copies, translation and representation. See below. |

Sources: [Trade Ministry company guidance](https://ticaret.gov.tr/ic-ticaret/sirketler/sirket-bilgiler), [national investment guide](https://www.invest.gov.tr/en/investmentguide/pages/establishing-a-business.aspx), [Istanbul’s 2026 registry tariff](https://www.ito.org.tr/documents/Ticaret-Sicil/onemli_bilgiler_ve_duyurular/harc.pdf), [Istanbul chamber dues](https://ito.org.tr/tr/hizmetler/aidat-islemleri), [Antalya’s 2026 tariff](https://www.atso.org.tr/tr/hizmetler/uye-rehberi/uye-ucretleri).

**Registration versus annual chamber dues:** registration is a one-time charge. Annual dues follow; **no separate annual dues are charged in the registration year**. Istanbul collects subsequent annual dues in June and October. Its 2026 ordinary capital/net-assets band containing ₺50,000 carries **₺3,500 annual dues**. There is also supplementary dues of **0.5% of commercial balance-sheet profit**, subject to statutory limits. These are ongoing costs, not another formation charge. [Istanbul dues rules](https://ito.org.tr/tr/hizmetler/aidat-islemleri)

These are components, **not an all-inclusive passport-translation quote**. The August 2026 amendment concerns travel allowances, not those components. I could not verify a complete notary bill for a particular founder. [2026 tariff](https://higm.adalet.gov.tr/Resimler/SayfaDokuman/202512301701273242026%20YILI%20NOTERL%C4%B0K%20%C3%9CCRET%20TAR%C4%B0FES%C4%B0.pdf), [amendment](https://higm.adalet.gov.tr/Resimler/SayfaDokuman/202608262203155332026%20YILI%20NOTERL%C4%B0K%20%C3%9CCRET%20TAR%C4%B0FES%C4%B0NDE%20DE%C4%9E%C4%B0%C5%9E%C4%B0KL%C4%B0K%20YAPILMASINA%20DA%C4%B0R%20TAR%C4%B0FE.pdf)

The listed Istanbul components total **₺8,495 + ₺2.48 × announcement words**, at minimum capital, **before** any additional signature declaration, foreign-document costs or professional services. 3. **The address**

**I could not verify a nationwide, unconditional “yes” for registering a limited company at either a home or virtual office. I also could not verify a universal requirement to sign a commercial lease.**

What the official evidence actually establishes:

- **Virtual offices:** GİB expressly recognises arrangements used by multiple unrelated individuals and legal entities, including mail handling and occasional meeting-room use. However, that ruling concerns **environmental-cleaning tax**, not blanket trade-registry acceptance or municipal licensing. It cannot support “every limited company may use any virtual office.” [GİB ruling](https://gib.gov.tr/mevzuat/kanun/449/ozelge/37289)
- **Home offices:** the Ministry’s opinion about home-office used-car trading requires licensing and rejects arrangements incompatible with that activity’s premises requirements. It establishes a **sector-specific legal restriction**, not a general ban on home-address companies. [Ministry opinion](https://webdosya.csb.gov.tr/db/yerelyonetimler/icerikler/07-02-2022_ik-nc--el-oto-galer--20241213134247.pdf)
- **Registration and operating permission must remain separate.** Registry or tax registration does not remove a licensing obligation.

I did **not** verify a named chamber/district comparison showing different acceptance of the same home-office or virtual-office facts. Therefore I cannot responsibly record “Istanbul allows it, district X does not.” This remains a verification gap, rather than established regional variation.

4. **Permission for foreign ownership**

**For an ordinary, unrestricted business, no separate foreign-investment permission is required merely to own a limited company.** Law 4875 replaces investment permission/approval with an information regime and provides freedom of investment and equal treatment, **subject expressly to special laws and treaties**. A foreigner can be the sole shareholder; there is no general Turkish-partner requirement or foreign-partner minimum percentage. **These are national rules, with no regional variation.** [Law 4875](https://www.karamandayatirim.gov.tr/assets/upload/dosyalar/dogrudan-yabanci-yatirimlar-kanunu.pdf), [official formation guide](https://www.invest.gov.tr/en/investmentguide/pages/establishing-a-business.aspx)

**Residence:** living abroad does not itself disqualify a founder. Istanbul’s formation checklist accommodates foreign founders and asks for the residence document additionally **if the foreign founder resides in Turkey**. That documentary distinction is not a requirement that every shareholder reside in Turkey. [Istanbul limited-company checklist](https://www.ito.org.tr/tr/hizmetler/ticaret-sicili-islemleri/tescil-islemleri/limited-sirketler)

**Do not publish “no reciprocity requirement” without qualifying the sector:**

- **Private security:** Law 5188, Article 5 expressly subjects foreign persons establishing security companies, and foreign companies providing security services, to **reciprocity**; operating permission is also required. [Police Directorate’s legislation compilation](https://www.egm.gov.tr/kurumlar/egm.gov.tr/IcSite/ozelguvenlik/Ozel-Guvenlik-Mevzuat-Kitabi-06_2023.pdf)
- **Broadcasting:** RTÜK states that direct foreign capital cannot exceed **50% of paid-up capital**. Broadcasters must also be **anonim şirket**, so a limited company cannot itself be the licensed broadcaster. This combines an ownership restriction with a company-form restriction. [RTÜK guidance](https://www.rtuk.gov.tr/izin-ve-tahsisler-dairesi-baskanligi/3923)

I verified those exceptions, **not an exhaustive list of restricted or closed sectors**. I cannot certify that every other sector is unrestricted.

5. **The five-Turkish-employees claim**

**Do not attach a five-Turkish-employee requirement to ordinary company formation or passive foreign ownership.** I found no separate general obligation requiring a limited company to employ five Turkish citizens simply because it exists or has a foreign shareholder.

The verified provision is specifically in **the foreign partner’s work-permit assessment criteria**, section C.1.1(b). It requires five Turkish citizens each month **from the beginning of the seventh month of the first work permit**—not the seventh month after incorporation.

Even within work-permit assessment, it is not universal: section C.1.2 exempts partners with a capital share of **US$100,000 or more** from those specified capital and employment criteria. Likewise, the **20% shareholding / ₺500,000 capital-share criteria** belong to work-permit assessment, not ordinary incorporation. **No regional variation.** [Labour Ministry criteria](https://csgb.gov.tr/uigm/calisma-izni/calisma-izni-degerlendirme-kriterleri/)

The defensible product rule is: **“Foreign ownership alone does not trigger the five-Turkish-employee condition. A foreign partner seeking a work permit may trigger it, subject to the applicable exemptions.”** I would not extend that into a claim that no sector-specific licence or other programme ever imposes staffing conditions.

# Starting a limited company in Turkey as a foreigner

**Agreed 2026-09-12**, after two turns in `talk/turkey/company-formation.md`.
Five overclaims found at sign-off, and one of them had a threshold **backwards**.
Every sentence below is the corrected one, and none may be softened, sharpened
or given colour when it becomes a guide.

## What a reader is told

**Can you even own one?** For an ordinary business, yes, and you need no
investment permission to do it. A foreigner can be the sole shareholder, there
is no Turkish partner requirement and no minimum foreign shareholding. Living
abroad does not disqualify you. Some sectors are different: **private security**
is subject to reciprocity and needs operating permission, and **broadcasting**
caps direct foreign capital at half the paid-up capital and must be an *anonim
şirket* rather than a limited one. We verified those two; we did not check every
sector, so check yours.

**The five Turkish employees.** This is the thing that stops people, and it is
misplaced. **There is no obligation on a company to employ five Turkish citizens
simply because it exists or has a foreign shareholder.** The five belongs to the
work permit assessment for a foreign partner, and it starts from the **seventh
month of that first work permit**, not from incorporation. A partner with a
capital share of US$100,000 or more is exempt from it.

**What it costs.** The **minimum** capital is 50,000 lira. For cash capital, no
statutory payment is required before registration; the full subscribed amount
must be paid within **twenty-four months after registration**, or earlier under
the payment schedule in the articles or set by the managers. This rule is
national. The capital is your company's money, not a fee.

The state's own formation fee is **zero**: formation is exempt from the registry
*harç*. What you actually pay is the **Competition Authority levy at 0.04 per
cent** of subscribed capital, which is 20 lira at the minimum; the **Trade
Registry Gazette at 2.48 lira per word** of your announcement; and your
chamber's own charges, **which differ by city**.

Istanbul's published 2026 tariff lists 2,160 lira for formation certification,
2,760 for opening-book certification and 250 for publication expenses; chamber
registration for a new capital company is 3,305 lira. These are verified tariff
entries, not a confirmed complete bill: a signature declaration is separately
listed at 1,720 lira, and we could not verify when it is additionally charged.
Antalya's equivalent new registration is **4,875 lira**.

We verified national notarial tariff components, not an all-inclusive formation
quote: the notary's fee is 30 per cent of the applicable statutory notarial
*harç*, minimum 58.82 lira; writing and copying are 80.68 lira per chargeable
page; translation under Article 4 is 667.67 lira per page, halved for pages
containing **ten lines or fewer**.

Chamber registration is once. Annual dues follow, and none are charged in the
year you register. Istanbul assesses annual dues by registered capital **or net
assets**; its 2026 band from 25,001 to 250,000 lira carries **3,500 lira** a
year, collected in June and October, with supplementary dues of 0.5 per cent of
**commercial balance-sheet profit**, within statutory limits.

**What happens after registration, which is where the deadlines are.** Your tax
registration is transmitted by the registry, so you do not file a separate
commencement notification the way a sole trader does; check that it has actually
been established. Apply for **electronic tax notifications within fifteen days**
of commencement. Get your **tax certificate within a month** of your tax
liability starting, then renew it by **31 May** each year. Your SGK workplace
registration is created automatically by the registry-based formation. An
employee must normally be registered **at least a day before they start**.

**The municipal licence has no grace period.** If your premises and activity
need an opening and operating licence, you need it **before you open**, not
thirty days after you register, and premises operating without one can be closed
and fined.

A **2026 change** worth knowing: companies registered from 1 January 2026 keep
their share register and general meeting minutes electronically in the **ETDS**
system, opened automatically with registration.

**Where your company can live.** We could not verify a nationwide yes or no on
registering at a home address or a virtual office, and we could not verify a
universal requirement to hold a commercial lease. What is clear is that
registering a company and being allowed to operate from a place are two
different permissions, and some activities have premises requirements that a
home or a mailbox cannot meet. We did not find a verified comparison of two
named cities accepting different facts, so we are not claiming one.

**A bookkeeper.** We could not establish that every new limited company must
retain an external accountant within a fixed period. National rules require
professional signatures for specified tax returns from covered taxpayers,
subject to exemptions. The published 2026 framework includes corporate taxpayers
whose previous-year assets **do not exceed** 115,209,000 lira and net sales **do
not exceed** 230,359,000 lira. It does not establish that professional
involvement becomes mandatory only above a size threshold.

## What this means for the schema

**A second regional rule that carries money**, after Hamburg's €16: the chamber
charge differs by city, Istanbul 3,305 against Antalya 4,875, and that is a
published tariff rather than an office habit. Turkey's law is still national
throughout; what is regional here is a **fee set by a local body**, which is the
same shape as Hamburg and the same argument for SB-168.

**Everything with a date in it changes on a schedule**: the minimum wage in
January, the chamber tariffs annually, the notary tariff annually. The guide's

# Starting a limited company in Turkey as a foreigner

**Agreed 2026-09-12**, after two turns in `talk/turkey/company-formation.md`.

## What a reader is told

**Can you even own one?** For an ordinary business, yes, and you need no
investment permission to do it. A foreigner can be the sole shareholder, there
is no Turkish partner requirement and no minimum foreign shareholding. Living
abroad does not disqualify you. Some sectors are different: **private security**
is subject to reciprocity and needs operating permission, and **broadcasting**
caps direct foreign capital at half[^law6112-19-1-f-half][^rtuk-faq-24-half] the paid-up capital and must be an *anonim
şirket*[^law6112-19-1-a-anonim] rather than a limited one. We verified those two; we did not check every
sector, so check yours.

**The five Turkish employees.** This is the thing that stops people, and it is
misplaced. **There is no obligation on a company to employ five Turkish citizens
simply because it exists or has a foreign shareholder.** The five belongs to the
work permit assessment for a foreign partner[^csgb-criteria-c-1-1-b], and it starts from the **seventh
month of that first work permit**[^csgb-criteria-c-1-1-b], not from incorporation. A partner with a
capital share of US$100,000 or more[^csgb-criteria-c-1-2] is exempt from it.

**What it costs.** The **minimum** capital is 50,000 lira[^cb-karar-7887-limited-50000][^rg-2023-11-26-duzeltme-7887][^ttk-580-minimum-capital][^ticaret-limited-50000]. For cash capital, no
statutory payment is required before registration[^ttk-585-1-limited]; the full subscribed amount
must be paid within **twenty-four months after registration**[^ttk-344-1-twenty-four-months][^ttk-585-1-limited][^ito-limited-24-months], or earlier under
the payment schedule in the articles or set by the managers[^ito-limited-24-months]. This rule is
national. The capital is your company's money, not a fee.

The state's own formation fee is **zero**[^law492-123-formation-exempt][^ito-harc-formation-exempt]: formation is exempt from the registry
*harç*. What you actually pay is the **Competition Authority levy at 0.04 per
cent**[^law4054-39-c-levy][^ito-limited-levy] of subscribed capital, which is 20 lira at the minimum[^ito-ltd-cost-sheet-levy-20]; the **Trade
Registry Gazette at 2.48 lira per word**[^ito-harc-gazette-2-48] of your announcement; and your
chamber's own charges, **which differ by city**.

Istanbul's published 2026 tariff lists 2,160 lira for formation certification[^ito-harc-formation-certification-2160],
2,760 for opening-book certification[^ito-harc-opening-books-2760] and 250 for publication expenses[^ito-harc-publication-expenses-250]; chamber
registration for a new capital company is 3,305 lira[^ito-ltd-cost-sheet-registration][^ito-dues-new-company-3305]. These are verified tariff
entries, not a confirmed complete bill: a signature declaration is separately
listed at 1,720 lira[^ito-harc-signature-declaration-1720], and we could not verify when it is additionally charged.
Antalya's equivalent new registration is **4,875 lira**[^atso-new-registration-4875].

We verified national notarial tariff components, not an all-inclusive formation
quote: the notary's fee is 30 per cent[^noter-2026-art1-thirty-percent] of the applicable statutory notarial
*harç*, minimum 58.82 lira[^noter-2026-art1-minimum]; writing and copying are 80.68 lira per chargeable
page[^noter-2026-art3-writing]; translation under Article 4 is 667.67 lira per page[^noter-2026-art4-translation], halved for pages
containing **ten lines or fewer**[^noter-2026-art4-short-pages].

Istanbul's chamber charges a registration fee[^ito-ltd-cost-sheet-registration]. Annual dues follow, and none are
charged in the year you register[^ito-dues-registration-year]. Istanbul assesses annual dues by registered
capital **or net assets**[^ito-dues-basis]; its 2026 band from 25,001 to 250,000 lira carries

**What happens after registration, which is where the deadlines are.** Your tax
registration is transmitted by the registry, so you do not file a separate
commencement notification the way a sole trader does; check that it has actually
been established. Apply for **electronic tax notifications within fifteen days**[^gib-brochure-2026-e-tebligat][^gib-brochure-2025-e-tebligat]
of commencement. Get your **tax certificate within a month**[^gib-brochure-2026-certificate-month][^gib-brochure-2025-certificate-month] of your tax
liability starting, then renew it by **31 May** each year[^gib-brochure-2026-certificate-may][^gib-rights-guide-31-may]. Your SGK workplace
registration is created automatically by the registry-based formation. An
employee must normally be registered **at least a day before they start**[^sgk-employer-one-day][^sgb5510-8-1-before-start][^sgb5510-7-1a-start].

**The municipal licence has no grace period.** If your premises and activity
need an opening and operating licence, you need it **before you open**[^isyeri-ruhsat-reg-6-before-open][^ticaret-bakkallik-before-open], and
premises operating without one can be closed and fined.

A **2026 change** worth knowing: companies registered from 1 January 2026[^ticaret-etds-2026] keep
their share register and general meeting minutes electronically in the **ETDS**
system, opened automatically with registration[^ticaret-etds-2026].

**Where your company can live.** We could not verify a nationwide yes or no on
registering at a home address or a virtual office, and we could not verify a
universal requirement to hold a commercial lease. What is clear is that
registering a company and being allowed to operate from a place are two
different permissions[^isyeri-ruhsat-reg-6-before-open], and some activities have premises requirements that a
home or a mailbox cannot meet. We did not find a verified comparison of two
named cities accepting different facts, so we are not claiming one.

**A bookkeeper.** We could not establish that every new limited company must
retain an external accountant within a fixed period. National rules require
professional signatures for specified tax returns from covered taxpayers,
subject to exemptions. We could not verify the applicable 2026 asset and
net-sales thresholds from a government source. The available evidence does not
establish that professional involvement becomes mandatory only above a size
threshold.

# Starting a limited company in Turkey as a foreigner

**Agreed 2026-09-12**, after two turns in `talk/turkey/company-formation.md`.
Five overclaims found at sign-off, and one of them had a threshold **backwards**.
Every sentence below is the corrected one, and none may be softened, sharpened
or given colour when it becomes a guide.

## What a reader is told

**Can you even own one?** For an ordinary business, yes, and you need no
investment permission to do it. A foreigner can be the sole shareholder, there
is no Turkish partner requirement and no minimum foreign shareholding. Living
abroad does not disqualify you. Some sectors are different: **private security**
is subject to reciprocity and needs operating permission, and **broadcasting**
caps direct foreign capital at half[^law6112-19-1-f-half][^rtuk-faq-24-half] the paid-up capital and must be an *anonim
şirket*[^law6112-19-1-a-anonim] rather than a limited one. We verified those two; we did not check every
sector, so check yours.

**The five Turkish employees.** This is the thing that stops people, and it is
misplaced. **There is no obligation on a company to employ five Turkish citizens
simply because it exists or has a foreign shareholder.** The five belongs to the
work permit assessment for a foreign partner[^csgb-criteria-c-1-1-b], and it starts from the **seventh
month of that first work permit**[^csgb-criteria-c-1-1-b], not from incorporation. A partner with a
capital share of US$100,000 or more[^csgb-criteria-c-1-2] is exempt from it.

**What it costs.** The **minimum** capital is 50,000 lira[^cb-karar-7887-limited-50000][^rg-2023-11-26-duzeltme-7887][^ttk-580-minimum-capital][^ticaret-limited-50000]. For cash capital, no
statutory payment is required before registration[^ttk-585-1-limited]; the full subscribed amount
must be paid within **twenty-four months after registration**[^ttk-344-1-twenty-four-months][^ttk-585-1-limited][^ito-limited-24-months], or earlier under
the payment schedule in the articles or set by the managers[^ito-limited-24-months]. This rule is
national. The capital is your company's money, not a fee.

The state's own formation fee is **zero**[^law492-123-formation-exempt][^ito-harc-formation-exempt]: formation is exempt from the registry
*harç*. What you actually pay is the **Competition Authority levy at 0.04 per
cent**[^law4054-39-c-levy][^ito-limited-levy] of subscribed capital, which is 20 lira at the minimum[^ito-ltd-cost-sheet-levy-20]; the **Trade
Registry Gazette at 2.48 lira per word**[^ito-harc-gazette-2-48] of your announcement; and your
chamber's own charges, **which differ by city**.

Istanbul's published 2026 tariff lists 2,160 lira for formation certification[^ito-harc-formation-certification-2160],
2,760 for opening-book certification[^ito-harc-opening-books-2760] and 250 for publication expenses[^ito-harc-publication-expenses-250]; chamber
registration for a new capital company is 3,305 lira[^ito-ltd-cost-sheet-registration][^ito-dues-new-company-3305]. These are verified tariff
entries, not a confirmed complete bill: a signature declaration is separately
listed at 1,720 lira[^ito-harc-signature-declaration-1720] in the general tariff, while the limited-company formation
cost sheet includes signature declarations in its 2,160 lira service fee[^ito-ltd-cost-sheet-service-fee-2160].
Antalya's equivalent new registration is **4,875 lira**[^atso-new-registration-4875].

We verified national notarial tariff components, not an all-inclusive formation
quote: the notary's fee is 30 per cent[^noter-2026-art1-thirty-percent] of the applicable statutory notarial
*harç*, minimum 58.82 lira[^noter-2026-art1-minimum]; writing and copying are 80.68 lira per chargeable
page[^noter-2026-art3-writing]; translation under Article 4 is 667.67 lira per page[^noter-2026-art4-translation], halved for pages
containing **ten lines or fewer**[^noter-2026-art4-short-pages].

Istanbul's chamber charges a registration fee[^ito-ltd-cost-sheet-registration]. Annual dues follow, and none are
charged in the year you register[^ito-dues-registration-year]. Istanbul assesses annual dues by registered
capital **or net assets**[^ito-dues-basis]; its 2026 band from 25,001 to 250,000 lira carries
**3,500 lira** a year[^ito-dues-band-2], collected in June and October[^ito-dues-instalments], with supplementary dues of
0.5 per cent of **commercial balance-sheet profit**[^ito-dues-supplementary], within statutory limits.

**What happens after registration, which is where the deadlines are.** Your tax
registration is transmitted by the registry, so you do not file a separate
commencement notification the way a sole trader does; check that it has actually
been established. Apply for **electronic tax notifications within fifteen days**[^gib-brochure-2026-e-tebligat][^gib-brochure-2025-e-tebligat]
of commencement. Get your **tax certificate within a month**[^gib-brochure-2026-certificate-month][^gib-brochure-2025-certificate-month] of your tax
liability starting, then renew it by **31 May** each year[^gib-brochure-2026-certificate-may][^gib-teblig-408-31-may][^gib-rights-guide-31-may], or, for a special
accounting period, within one month after the declaration deadline[^gib-teblig-408-special-period]. Your SGK
workplace registration is created automatically by the registry-based
formation. An employee must normally be registered **at least a day before
they start**[^sgk-employer-one-day][^sgb5510-8-1-before-start][^sgb5510-7-1a-start].

**The municipal licence has no grace period.** If your premises and activity
need an opening and operating licence, you need it **before you open**[^isyeri-ruhsat-reg-6-before-open][^ticaret-bakkallik-before-open], and
premises operating without one can be closed.

A **2026 change** worth knowing: companies registered from 1 January 2026[^ticaret-etds-2026] keep
their share register and general meeting minutes electronically in the **ETDS**
system, opened automatically with registration[^ticaret-etds-2026].

**Where your company can live.** We could not verify a nationwide yes or no on
registering at a home address or a virtual office, and we could not verify a
universal requirement to hold a commercial lease. What is clear is that
registering a company and being allowed to operate from a place are two
different permissions[^isyeri-ruhsat-reg-6-before-open], and some activities have premises requirements that a
home or a mailbox cannot meet. We did not find a verified comparison of two
named cities accepting different facts, so we are not claiming one.

**A bookkeeper.** We could not establish that every new limited company must
retain an external accountant within a fixed period. National rules require
professional signatures for specified tax returns from covered taxpayers,
subject to exemptions. We could not verify the applicable 2026 asset and
net-sales thresholds from a government source. The available evidence does not
establish that professional involvement becomes mandatory only above a size
threshold.

The rule case is health insurance in Turkey for a foreigner: both the private policy the residence permit wants and SGK general health insurance.

Settled elsewhere and not to be re-derived: for a short-term residence permit the coverage requirement can be met by a provincial SGK certificate under a bilateral agreement, an SGK provision document, an SGK general health insurance application certificate, or a private policy meeting the official minimum standard; applicants under eighteen and over sixty-five are exempt from obtaining it though existing cover must still be submitted; and a foreigner resident continuously for a year may request SGK general health insurance.

Six things.

1. THE PRIVATE POLICY'S MINIMUM STANDARD. There is an insurance regulator circular on health insurance for visa and residence permit applications, amended in 2024. What does it actually require: minimum coverage amounts, what must be covered, any deductible or co-payment limits, and must the insurer be licensed in Turkey? If a policy bought abroad can satisfy it, say so, because that is what most people arrive holding.

2. THE ONE YEAR FOR SGK, precisely. One year of continuous residence: measured from what, and does time on a short-term permit count? Does leaving the country break continuity, and if so after how many days? And once eligible, is joining a right or is it assessed?

4. WHO IS EXCLUDED. I understand somebody who is covered as a dependent, or who is employed and therefore insured through work, cannot join general health insurance separately. 5. THE GAP THAT COSTS MONEY. A person arrives, buys the cheap private policy that satisfies the permit, and then needs actual medical care. ## Answered

1. **Private policy minimum standard**

SEDDK Circular **2024/34**, amending **2016/16**, took effect on **1 April 2025**. These are annual minimum limits; percentages below are the insured person’s share. [SEDDK announcement](https://seddk.gov.tr/upload/Bas%C4%B1n%20duyurusu.27-12-24.pdf)

| Treatment provider | Outpatient limit / patient share | Inpatient limit / patient share |
|---|---:|---:|
| Insurer-contracted providers | ₺15,000 / 20% | Unlimited / 0% |
| Non-contracted providers and public hospitals outside Annex 1 | ₺15,000 / 40% | ₺150,000 / 20% |
| Public hospitals named in Annex 1 | ₺15,000 / 20% | ₺250,000 / 0% |

Treatment for sudden illnesses and ailments cannot be excluded, **subject to Article 4/A**, which preserves waiting-period provisions. I could not verify a separate monetary deductible allowance or ceiling. The percentages above are the circular’s cost-sharing standard. [2024/34](https://www.seddk.gov.tr/upload/Vize%20ve%20%C4%B0kamet%20%C4%B0zni%20Taleplerinde%20Yapt%C4%B1r%C4%B1lacak%20Sa%C4%9Fl%C4%B1k%20Sigortalar%C4%B1na%20%C4%B0li%C5%9Fkin%20Genelgede%20De%C4%9Fi%C5%9Fiklik%20Yap%C4%B1lmas%C4%B1na%20Dair%20Genelge%20%282024-34%29.pdf)

For an application **made in Turkey**, Article 4 requires an insurer authorised for the relevant insurance branch in Turkey, including the Turkish establishment of a foreign insurer. A policy issued abroad by a foreign insurer is expressly acceptable **when the residence application is made abroad**, provided it supplies the required cover in Turkey. But Migration Management currently says residence applications are not yet accepted abroad. **An existing overseas policy therefore cannot be assumed acceptable for an application after arrival.** [2016/16, Article 4](https://seddk.gov.tr/upload/Genelge%20%282016-16%29.pdf), [Migration Management FAQ](https://www.goc.gov.tr/ikamet-sss)

These requirements are national: **no regional variation**.

2. **The SGK year**

Official SGK guidance describes residence **under a residence permit**, exceeding one year. It does not restrict this to a long-term permit. Including short-term permits is a reading of that general wording; **I did not find an explicit official short-term-permit confirmation**. [SGK guide, pp. 22–23](https://www.sgk.gov.tr/Download/DownloadFileStatics?d=YAYINLARIMIZ&f=GSS_Sistemi_Kitabi_TR.pdf)

**I could not officially verify the precise starting-date calculation or an absence threshold.** A third-party-hosted copy of SGK Circular 2019/17, §3.5.4, instructs offices to examine the residence periods recorded on the permit and accepts a continuous year completed in an earlier period. I could not authenticate that detail against an official-hosted copy. It does not establish a permissible number of days abroad. I would not publish an immigration-law absence threshold as an SGK rule. [Copy examined](https://www.krm.com.tr/upload/data/files/mevzuat/5510_sosyal_sigortalar_ve_genel_saglik_sigortasi_kanunu/genelgeler/genel_saglik_sigortasi_tescil_ve_prim_islemleri_hakkinda_genelge.pdf)

Once the legal conditions are met, registration on request is a **statutory entitlement subject to eligibility verification**, rather than an unrestricted office discretion. Article 61(1)(c) starts GSS on **the day after the request**, not automatically on the anniversary. [Law 6486, Article 2](https://www5.tbmm.gov.tr/tutanaklar/KANUNLAR_KARARLAR/kanuntbmmc097/kanuntbmmc097/kanuntbmmc09706486.pdf)

This is a national rule; I verified no provincial legal difference.

3. **2026 cost**

For the ordinary foreign-resident category, **60(1)(d)**:

- 2026 gross monthly minimum wage: **₺33,030**.
- Contribution base: twice that amount, **₺66,060**.
- Premium: **12% × ₺66,060 = ₺7,927.20 monthly**.

The double-minimum-wage basis and equivalent 24% of minimum wage appear in SGK’s guide; the Labour Ministry supplies the 2026 wage. **I calculated ₺66,060 and ₺7,927.20 from those official inputs.** [SGK guide, pp. 24–26](https://www.sgk.gov.tr/Download/DownloadFileStatics?d=YAYINLARIMIZ&f=GSS_Sistemi_Kitabi_TR.pdf), [2026 minimum wage](https://csgb.gov.tr/poco-pages/asgari-ucret/)

The same calculation is printed in §4.2 of a copy of SGK Circular 2026/2 hosted by Ankara’s accountants’ chamber; I did not verify that copy on SGK’s own host. [Copy examined](https://asmmmo.org.tr/userfiles/others/files/Mvzt/Gnlg/5510/2026-02.pdf)

**No regional variation.** This figure is not the separate foreign-student rate.

4. **Exclusions and starting employment**

Employees insured through work and eligible dependants are **already within the GSS system**. The issue is exclusion from separate foreign-resident registration under 60(1)(d), rather than exclusion from healthcare insurance altogether. SGK also identifies foreign statutory insurance as relevant to eligibility; the detailed interaction with foreign coverage requires checking the applicable status. [SGK guide](https://www.sgk.gov.tr/Download/DownloadFileStatics?d=YAYINLARIMIZ&f=GSS_Sistemi_Kitabi_TR.pdf)

For ordinary employment subject to Turkish insurance, insurance starts **when work starts**, under Article 7. There are exceptions: a worker temporarily posted under a social-security agreement can remain insured in the sending country. [Law 5510, Article 7](https://www.csgb.gov.tr/Media/eavld5wl/5510-sayl-kanun.pdf), [SGK posting guidance](https://www.sgk.gov.tr/Content/Post/194c7041-1d05-48a1-bba8-6a46d785b880/Gecici-Gorevlendirme-2024-12-24-02-47-24)

**First-day insurance is not always first-day routine treatment entitlement.** SGK normally requires 30 contribution days in the preceding year, but expressly waives that requirement for transitions between insurance categories and from dependant coverage. [SGK healthcare entitlement guidance](https://www.sgk.gov.tr/Content/Post/4d85599f-e810-4755-a81b-02be5c253d60/Yurtici-2022-08-23-04-08-30)

The 2019/17 copy says separate 60(1)(d) registration ends **the day before** another GSS category or dependant status begins. **I could not independently verify that exact cancellation-date instruction from an official-hosted source.** [Copy examined, §3.5.4](https://www.krm.com.tr/upload/data/files/mevzuat/5510_sosyal_sigortalar_ve_genel_saglik_sigortasi_kanunu/genelgeler/genel_saglik_sigortasi_tescil_ve_prim_islemleri_hakkinda_genelge.pdf)

5. **The treatment gap**

A compliant permit policy must provide **actual outpatient and inpatient treatment cover** at the floor in item 1. Calling it merely paperwork would be wrong. **I could not verify what cheap policies “typically” pay in practice from official evidence.** The limits, patient shares, provider network and permitted waiting periods matter.

I located the official **2022/7** waiting-period amendment but could not read its operative text sufficiently to verify the numerical limits. I therefore cannot responsibly promise immediate cover for every illness.

**Emergency care is not universally free to an uninsured foreigner.** For visitors within the 2025 tourist-health regulation, Article 10 expressly makes emergency services and emergency transport chargeable. Treatment must be provided unconditionally and without delay; collection happens afterwards. Traffic-accident treatment is an express exception: SGK pays under the applicable legislation, and the patient cannot personally be charged. [2025 regulation, Article 10](https://dosyahastane.saglik.gov.tr/Eklenti/541143/0/saglik-turizm-yonetmeligi-28042025pdf.pdf)

For residence-permit holders without GSS, an official state-hospital pricing page says services are charged under the public healthcare tariff. **That page does not separately resolve every emergency exemption for settled residents**, so I would not extend the visitor provision to every foreign status without qualification. [State-hospital pricing guidance](https://necipfazildh.saglik.gov.tr/TR,371447/pricing.html)

6. **Provincial variation**

**No provincial variation in the verified legal eligibility rules, premium formula or private-policy minimum standard.**

There is, however, a **nationally prescribed hospital-specific distinction**: Annex 1 names 20 public hospitals receiving the special coverage tier. Store that as a legal provider distinction, not provincial office practice. Insurer-contracted versus non-contracted providers are another relevant distinction. [SEDDK announcement](https://seddk.gov.tr/upload/Bas%C4%B1n%20duyurusu.27-12-24.pdf)

For office routing and documents, the 2019/17 copy specifies the SGK provincial directorate/social-security centre for the person’s residence, a written application, the Annex 6 undertaking and the residence permit. **I could not verify that checklist on an official host, or verify province-specific additions or different office behaviour.** “No verified local difference” does not establish that every counter requests identical paperwork.

## What a reader is told

**The policy your permit needs has a legal minimum.** The insurance regulator
sets it, in circular **2016/16 as amended by 2024/34**, in force since **1 April
2025**. Per year:

| Where you are treated | Outpatient | Your share | Inpatient | Your share |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| A provider your insurer has contracted with | ₺15,000 | 20% | unlimited | 0% |
| Non-contracted, and public hospitals outside Annex 1 | ₺15,000 | 40% | ₺150,000 | 20% |
| The 20 public hospitals named in Annex 1 | ₺15,000 | 20% | ₺250,000 | 0% |

Treatment for sudden illness cannot be excluded, subject to the waiting-period
rules. We located the 2022/7 waiting-period amendment but could not read its
operative text, so we cannot promise immediate cover for every illness.

**Do not assume the policy you already own counts.** For an application made
inside Turkey, the policy must be **concluded in Turkey** with an insurer
authorised for the relevant branch, including an authorised Turkish
establishment of a foreign insurer. The circular allows a policy concluded with
an insurer abroad for a residence application made **abroad**, provided it
supplies the required minimum cover in Turkey; migration management says
residence applications **are not yet accepted abroad**. So do not assume the travel
policy you flew in with is the one your permit accepts.

**SGK, and the year.** A foreigner with **more than a year of continuous
residence under a residence permit** may apply for foreign-resident general
health insurance, subject to the other eligibility conditions, including their
existing insurance status. Official guidance says residence under a residence
permit and does not restrict it to a particular permit type. We could not verify
exactly how the year is calculated or how many days abroad break it, so ask SGK
rather than trusting a number you read.

Joining is not automatic on your anniversary: for an eligible applicant, **GSS
registration starts the day after the request**. Routine treatment entitlement
normally also requires **thirty contribution days** in the preceding year and,
for this category, no outstanding premium-related debt, subject to statutory
exceptions.

**What it costs, and it is not small.** The premium is **12% of twice the gross
minimum wage**. With the 2026 minimum wage of ₺33,030 that is a base of ₺66,060
and a premium of **₺7,927.20 a month**. Those last two figures were calculated
here from the official rate and the official wage; the same calculation also
appears in a copy of an SGK circular hosted by an accountants' chamber, which we
could not verify on SGK's own host.

**If you get a job, this changes.** Employment insurance starts on the day you
start work (Law 5510, Art 7). Being insured from day one is not the same as
being entitled to routine treatment from day one: SGK normally wants thirty days
of contributions in the preceding year, though it waives that when you move
between categories or from being a dependant. Somebody posted to Turkey under a
social security agreement may stay insured in their home country instead.

**Emergency care is not automatically free to an uninsured foreign visitor.**
Under the 2025 regulation covering visitors, emergency services and emergency
transport are **chargeable**. The regulation requires treatment to be provided
unconditionally and without delay, with collection afterwards; that is a legal
duty on the provider, not a guarantee of how a given hospital behaves. The
exception is a **traffic accident**, where SGK pays under the applicable
legislation and the patient cannot be charged personally. For a residence permit
holder without SGK, an official state-hospital pricing page says services are
charged under the public tariff; that page does not resolve every emergency
exemption for settled residents, and we did not verify them.

**By province.** The verified legal rules are national, with no provincial
variation. We could not verify whether office procedures or additional document
requests differ locally. For the private permit-policy minimum standard, Annex 1
public hospitals receive more favourable minimum coverage terms than non-listed
public hospitals, and provider network status affects the required coverage
terms.

## Answered

**I would not publish the claim as written.** The 20-working-day rule is verified for identified categories, but physical presence alone is not a verified basis for applying it to every foreigner. First registration and notification after moving also have different triggers.

**1. Who is covered**

The legal routes must be kept separate:

| Category | Verified basis |
|---|---|
| Foreigners holding residence permits | Migration’s general information page expressly requires notification of address changes during the permit’s validity. The implementing regulation for Law 6458, Article 23(2), requires notification to the governorate within 20 working days. |
| Foreigners entered in the foreigners register | Population Services Law 5490, Article 8, provides for registration of holders of permits issued for **at least 90 days**, and voluntary registration of other lawfully present foreigners. NVI identifies Article 22 of the **Regulation on Keeping Population Records of Foreigners Residing in Turkey** as the address-change provision. |
| Work-permit holders | Included in the initial address-registration requirement under Article 22(12) of the Law 6458 implementing regulation. I have not independently verified the complete legal chain for their subsequent address changes. |
| International-protection applicants and status holders | Law 6458, Article **90(1)(c)** expressly requires address-change notification within 20 working days. This includes applicants awaiting a decision. |
| Foreigners within the Temporary Protection Regulation | Article **33(2)(d)** expressly requires address-change notification within 20 working days. |

Sources: [Migration’s general information](https://www.goc.gov.tr/ikamet-genel-bilgiler), [Interior Ministry inspection guide, §§04.05–04.06](https://www.icisleri.gov.tr/kurumlar/icisleri.gov.tr/IcSite/mulkiyeteftis/Teftis-Rehberleri/IL-GOC-IDARESI-MUDURLUGU-TEFTIS-REHBERI.pdf), [Parliament’s enacted Law 6458, including its amendment of Article 8](https://cdn.tbmm.gov.tr/KKBSPublicFile/D24/Y2/T1/KanunMetni/3b3cee0d-dae5-4b98-80dc-9f3c62607e58.html), [NVI’s explanation of Article 22](https://www.nvi.gov.tr/istanbul/adres-islemleri), [Temporary Protection Regulation](https://www.goc.gov.tr/kurumlar/goc.gov.tr/gecicikorumayonetmeligi.pdf).

**The 90-day threshold is a register-entry provision, not a verified universal exemption from address duties for everybody else.** I could not retrieve the consolidated foreigners-records regulation or establish an exhaustive current list of exemptions. That part remains unsettled.

These are national provisions: **no regional variation in the stated 20-working-day deadlines.**

**2. When the clock starts**

- **Changing an existing residence:** from the actual move/change of residence. NVI explicitly compares the notification date with the **moving date**. Signing a lease alone is not the stated trigger. [NVI FAQ](https://nvi.gov.tr/sss-adres-hizmetleri)
- **First registration following a permit obtained abroad:** from entry into Turkey.
- **First registration following a permit obtained inside Turkey:** from **delivery of the permit document to the person**, under implementing-regulation Article 22(12). The Interior Ministry’s inspection guide reproduces this distinction. [Inspection guide, §04.05.10](https://www.icisleri.gov.tr/kurumlar/icisleri.gov.tr/IcSite/mulkiyeteftis/Teftis-Rehberleri/IL-GOC-IDARESI-MUDURLUGU-TEFTIS-REHBERI.pdf)
- **Specific exception:** for humanitarian residence-permit holders, Law 6458 Article 46(2) says registration within 20 working days of **issuance**. [Law 6458](https://cdn.tbmm.gov.tr/KKBSPublicFile/D24/Y2/T1/KanunMetni/3b3cee0d-dae5-4b98-80dc-9f3c62607e58.html)

**Before a first permit is granted, an address duty does exist:** applicants must supply accurate, complete accommodation-address information; a fixed home or accommodation establishment can be given. That declaration must not be confused with completed registration in the Address Registration System. I could not verify a general requirement that an otherwise unregistered first-time applicant complete that registration within 20 working days of entry, signing a lease, or moving in. [Migration’s general information](https://www.goc.gov.tr/ikamet-genel-bilgiler)

1. Book an address-registration appointment through `randevu.goc.gov.tr`.
2. For a rented empty address, prepare:

- **False address declaration: TRY 17,051.**

## What a reader is told

**Who this applies to.** Residence-permit holders, international-protection
applicants and status holders, and temporary-protection beneficiaries must
report address changes within **20 working days**[^yukk-reg-23-2-twenty-working-days][^nvi-faq-residence-permit-20-days][^law6458-90-1-c-twenty-working-days][^yukk-reg-110-3-twenty-working-days][^gk-reg-33-2-d-twenty-working-days]. An initial
address-registration duty for work-permit holders is verified[^yukk-reg-22-12-delivered]; their
subsequent-move rule was not independently established. For someone here solely
on a visa or visa exemption, outside those categories, we could not verify the
same registration and change-notification duty.

Article 8 of Population Services Law 5490[^law5490-8-foreigners-register] authorises the Interior Ministry to
assign identity numbers to foreigners covered by Law 6458 and record them in the
foreigners register; diplomatic mission members are outside this provision. The
validity of foreigner identity numbers, the documents required during and after
an application, and other details are determined by an Interior Ministry
regulation.

**When the clock starts.** Moving house: from the day you move[^nvi-faq-moving-date], not the day you
sign the lease. A first permit issued abroad: from the day you enter Turkey[^law6458-26-2-from-entry][^yukk-reg-22-12-delivered]. An
ordinary first residence permit issued in Turkey: the regulation starts the
period when **the permit document is delivered**[^yukk-reg-22-12-delivered][^teftis-04-05-10]. A humanitarian residence
permit: the statutory period starts **on issuance**[^law6458-46-2-on-issuance][^yukk-reg-44-5-on-issuance]. The national short-term
application checklist instead says "following approval"[^eikamet-checklist-after-approval]; that wording conflicts
with the regulation's delivery trigger, and we are not hiding the conflict.

**Your application is not your registration.** Do not assume that giving your
address in e-İkamet completes address registration. The national short-term
application checklist expressly requires first applicants to complete
address-registration transactions after approval[^eikamet-checklist-after-approval]. We did not verify the
categorical claim that an application never propagates an address.

**One office or two.** The population directorate's FAQ says the population
directorate **or**[^nvi-faq-residence-permit-20-days] the provincial migration directorate. The Migration
Presidency's page reads as though you file with **both**. We found official
references to two potentially relevant instructions, an 8 December 2022
instruction no. 130606 and Article 33 of a 15 January 2025 implementation
directive[^teftis-04-05-10], but could not locate publicly accessible copies or verify whether
they resolve this conflict. Ask at whichever you go to first whether you also
need the other.

**Online.** An e-Devlet service for foreigners' address registration was
announced officially in 2022[^hatay-e-devlet-2022]. We could not confirm what it does today, nor
whether completing it there leaves no office visit owing.

**Bursa.** Bursa publishes an appointment-and-document procedure for address
registration, effective **1 June 2026**, notice dated 19 May 2026[^bursa-appointment-2026]: an
appointment through `randevu.goc.gov.tr`, and among the documents a **UETS
account**[^bursa-appointment-2026]. We could not verify a national UETS requirement for address
registration; the national requirement we verified concerns **renewal and
transfer applications** from 15 April 2024[^goc-uets-2024], and applicants under 18 need not
provide their own UETS account[^goc-uets-under-18]. We found no other matching provincial notice
dated 2025 or 2026, which is not the same as knowing Bursa is alone.

**If you are late.** 814 lira for late notification[^nvi-fines-2026], 17,051 lira for a false
address declaration, at 2026 rates[^nvi-fines-2026]. National figures, usually revised each
January[^nvi-istanbul-q9-annual][^law5326-17-7-annual].

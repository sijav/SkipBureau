## Answered

**1. Gewerbe versus freie Berufe**

There are **two classifications**, not one:

- **Tax law:** the Finanzamt applies federal income-tax law. §18 EStG covers specified activities—scientific, artistic, writing, teaching and education—named professions, and sufficiently similar professions. “Freelancer” is not itself a legal category. [§18 EStG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/__18.html)
- **Trade law:** the Gewerbeamt separately decides whether the activity requires Gewerbeanmeldung. The Bundesverwaltungsgericht expressly holds that tax classification does **not bind** the trade authority. Consequently, someone can be freiberuflich for tax purposes yet required to register under trade law. This is a distinction between legal regimes, not regional variation. [BVerwG, 8 C 7.12](https://www.bverwg.de/de/270213U8C7.12.0)

For a **self-employed software developer**, engineering-like work can qualify under §18. Both the actual work and the person’s knowledge matter. A relevant degree helps; an autodidact can qualify but must demonstrate comparable breadth and depth of knowledge. Merely programming professionally, holding a degree, or calling oneself an IT consultant does not settle both classifications. [BFH, III R 3/14](https://www.bundesfinanzhof.de/de/entscheidung/entscheidungen-online/detail/pdf/STRE201750088?type=1646225765)

For an uncertain case, submit the actual services, qualifications and representative projects to **both authorities**. A tax number or informal acceptance is not equivalent to a binding ruling. A *verbindliche Auskunft* under §89 AO can address a precisely described, prospective tax situation; it does not resolve the separate Gewerberecht question. [§89 AO](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ao_1977/__89.html)

**Getting it wrong:**

- **Failing to register an actual Gewerbe:** registration can be required retrospectively; missing or late notification can attract a fine up to **€1,000**. Tax reclassification can also produce Gewerbesteuer liabilities for earlier assessable years. For individuals and partnerships, the federal Gewerbeertrag allowance is **€24,500**; reclassification therefore does not automatically mean tax payable. [§146 GewO](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/gewo/__146.html), [§11 GewStG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/gewstg/__11.html)
- **Registering an activity that is actually non-commercial:** registration does not elect the person into commercial tax treatment. Ask the Gewerbeamt to correct the registration and the Finanzamt to apply the correct classification. **I could not verify a uniform official procedure guaranteeing retrospective cancellation or refunds of registration fees and chamber contributions.** Nor should registration be cancelled solely because the Finanzamt calls the activity freiberuflich.

The underlying tests are nationwide. **No Land-specific software-developer classification rule was verified.** Different assessments of an individual’s work are not, by themselves, different Land law.

**2. The Gewerbeanmeldung**

For an ordinary business establishment, notify the **Gewerbeamt or equivalent local trade authority where the establishment is located**—often part of an Ordnungsamt or Bezirksamt.

The federal deadline is **simultaneously with commencing the business**. There is no nationwide two-week or one-month grace period. [§14 GewO](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/gewo/__14.html)

These are the official fees published when checked on **12 September 2026**, for an ordinary individual registration:

| City | Published fee | Who sets the fee? |
|---|---:|---|
| Berlin | **€26**, or **€15 online** | Berlin’s Land administrative-fee ordinance |
| Hamburg | **€25** | Hamburg’s ordinance for economic-administration fees |
| Cologne | **€26** | **NRW’s** administrative-fee tariff, collected by Cologne |

Sources: [Berlin](https://service.berlin.de/dienstleistung/121921/), [Hamburg chamber](https://www.handelskammer-hamburg.de/gruendung-sicherung-nachfolge/gewerbe-an-um-melden/gewerbeanmeldung-6598040), [Hamburg legal basis](https://www.hamburg.de/service/info/11258072/n0/), [Cologne](https://www.stadt-koeln.de/service/produkte/00554/index.html).

Thus **“not federal” does not mean “set independently by the municipality.”** Legal form and additional representatives can change the charge.

**Online availability depends on the responsible authority and sometimes legal form.** Berlin expressly lists forms of business for which no online procedure exists. Bavaria’s official guidance says electronic submission is available **if the responsible authority provides it**. I could not verify universal online coverage in 2026. [Berlin](https://service.berlin.de/dienstleistung/121921/), [Bavarian service guidance](https://verwaltung.bund.de/leistungsverzeichnis/DE/leistung/99050012104000/herausgeber/BY-161/region/09)

A useful distinction between law and practice: Cologne describes possible warnings after four weeks and fine proceedings after six months. **Those are enforcement guidance, not extensions of the federal deadline.** [Cologne](https://www.stadt-koeln.de/service/produkte/00554/index.html)

**3. What registration sets off**

**Automatic transmission does not mean every recipient will contact you, or that every separate obligation is completed.**

The federal transmission scheme includes the Finanzamt, IHK, HWK, DGUV for forwarding to the responsible Berufsgenossenschaft, Bundesagentur für Arbeit, customs, immigration authorities, Knappschaft-Bahn-See, and relevant environmental, occupational-safety, weights-and-measures, food/veterinary and licensing authorities. Statistical offices also receive data. Transmission is limited by statutory purpose; recipients can waive regular transmission. The register-court provision specifically concerns certain **deregistrations**, not automatic company incorporation. [§14 GewO](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/gewo/__14.html)

For a small new business:

| Body | Compulsory? | First-year cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Finanzamt** | Tax registration and applicable tax duties are compulsory; this is not membership. | Registration is free. Taxes depend on the business and owner, not a fixed startup charge. |
| **IHK** | Normally compulsory for an ordinary commercial business within its statutory scope. No general opt-out. | Can be **€0** under the exemptions below; otherwise chamber-specific. |
| **HWK** | Compulsory where the business falls within the Handwerksordnung; mixed businesses can also have IHK obligations. | Qualifying first-time individual founders with Gewerbeertrag/profit no more than **€25,000** are exempt from basic and supplementary contributions in the registration year. Separate registration/service fees can remain. |
| **Berufsgenossenschaft** | Assignment to the competent statutory accident insurer is compulsory; the owner’s personal insurance is a separate question. | Depends on insured people, payroll, risk classification and insurer rules. No universal first-year price. |
| **Other receiving authorities** | Receiving the notification does not create a paid membership. | No blanket annual “recipient fee”; separate permits or employment obligations may generate costs. |

Sources: [tax registration](https://verwaltung.bund.de/leistungsverzeichnis/DE/leistung/99102019120000/herausgeber/HE-365573585/region/060000000000), [§2 IHKG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ihkg/__2.html), [HwO §§90 and 113](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/hwo/BJNR014110953.html), [DGUV](https://www.dguv.de/de/versicherung/unternehmensnummer/anmeldung/index.jsp).

**IHK membership and contribution exemption are different.** The principal federal contribution rules are:

- Non-Handelsregister individuals and partnerships: exemption where Gewerbeertrag—or the relevant business profit—does not exceed **€5,200**.
- Qualifying individuals: **no basic contribution or levy in the opening year and following year**, then no levy in years three and four, provided Gewerbeertrag/profit does not exceed **€25,000**.
- That founder relief requires no income from agriculture, commercial business or self-employment in the preceding five financial years, and no direct or indirect corporate holding exceeding 10% during that period.

These are **profit/earnings tests, not turnover tests**. §3 also permits a chamber to lower the exemption thresholds under a specified membership-financing condition; check its annual rules. [§3 IHKG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ihkg/__3.html)

Concrete 2026 example: IHK Rhein-Neckar charges a non-register business with **€10,000 profit €35 annually** if founder relief does not apply; no levy arises below its €15,340 allowance. With qualifying founder relief, the contribution is €0. These are chamber rules, not municipal charges. [IHK Rhein-Neckar](https://www.ihk.de/rhein-neckar/ueber-uns/beitrag/beitragsaetze-2017-2026-4653446)

For accident insurance, DGUV expressly says Gewerbeanmeldung satisfies the business-notification obligation; otherwise notification is due within **one week**. VBG says self-employed owners are not personally compulsorily insured with it and explains payroll-based contributions. **I did not verify an explicit official €0 quotation for the precise solo-software-developer case, so I would not yet publish that as a confirmed price.** [DGUV](https://www.dguv.de/de/versicherung/unternehmensnummer/anmeldung/index.jsp), [VBG](https://www.vbg.de/cms/unternehmer/startup)

**4. What a foreigner specifically needs**

For a third-country national, **the intended self-employment must be permitted before it is exercised**. A title restricted to a particular employment does not authorize another economic activity unless the competent authority permits it. Check the title and supplementary conditions, not merely whether its holder “has a work permit.” [§4a AufenthG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/aufenthg_2004/__4a.html)

A separate §21 residence permit is not always necessary: §21(6) permits authorization of self-employment while retaining another residence purpose. §21(5) separately addresses liberal professions. [§21 AufenthG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/aufenthg_2004/__21.html)

**Must authorization exist before the registration office can accept the notification? I would not publish an unconditional yes.**

Bavaria’s current service guidance requires a title permitting self-employment. However, published NRW administrative instructions expressly tell offices to request proof but **accept the notification even if it is not produced**, while warning that operating without permission is unlawful. The NRW text is old; I have not established uniform current handling across all Länder. This supports separating the document check and receipt of notification from the right to operate—not declaring a different substantive immigration entitlement in NRW. [Bavarian guidance](https://verwaltung.bund.de/leistungsverzeichnis/DE/leistung/99050012104000/herausgeber/BY-161/region/09), [NRW instructions, 5.4](https://recht.nrw.de/lrmb/verwaltungsvorschrift/allgemeine-verwaltungsvorschrift-zur-durchfuehrung-der-ssss-1415-und-55/)

If someone registers despite an employment-only restriction, **the Gewerbeschein does not remove that restriction**. Actually exercising unauthorized self-employment can be an administrative offence with a fine up to **€5,000** under §98(3)(1), (5). Registration alone must not be equated with committing that operating offence. I could not verify an automatic residence-permit cancellation consequence and would not claim one. [§98 AufenthG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/aufenthg_2004/__98.html)

**EU citizens:** self-employed establishment is covered by EU free movement; they do not need a German residence permit authorizing it. Ordinary Gewerbe registration and applicable professional requirements still apply. **No regional variation in that entitlement.** [§2 FreizügG/EU](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/freiz_gg_eu_2004/__2.html)

**5. Tax number and Kleinunternehmerregelung**

**Do not wait for the Finanzamt to send a questionnaire.** Within **one month of opening the business or starting the liberal-profession activity**, submit the *Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung* electronically, normally through ELSTER. A hardship exception to electronic submission is available on application. Gewerbeanmeldung does not replace this questionnaire. [§138 AO](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ao_1977/__138.html)

The Finanzamt examines the information, may request further documents, and then communicates the **Steuernummer in writing**. It uses the questionnaire to determine applicable taxes and anticipated payments. I could not verify a guaranteed initial letter or paper questionnaire sent to every founder. [Official tax-registration guidance](https://verwaltung.bund.de/leistungsverzeichnis/DE/leistung/99102019120000/herausgeber/HE-365573585/region/060000000000)

**Timing:** the official guidance checked says processing varies by Finanzamt. I did not verify a reliable numerical processing time for an ordinary business Steuernummer. The quoted “up to two weeks” on that page concerns **ELSTER account registration**, not tax-number issuance.

For a Germany-established business, the **2026 VAT exemption thresholds** are:

| Situation | Threshold |
|---|---|
| Business already operating in the preceding year | Previous calendar year’s relevant turnover **≤€25,000**, and current year’s **≤€100,000** |
| Year the business first starts | Relevant actual turnover **≤€25,000** |

These concern the statutory **Gesamtumsatz**, not profit. [§19 UStG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ustg_1980/__19.html), [BMF guidance, section 19.1](https://www.bundesfinanzministerium.de/Content/DE/Downloads/BMF_Schreiben/Steuerarten/Umsatzsteuer/Umsatzsteuer-Anwendungserlass/2025-03-18-sonderregelung-kleinunternehmer.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=4)

The transaction that takes turnover over the applicable current-year ceiling is itself no longer exempt, in full—not merely its excess portion.

**It is a choice to waive the exemption.** For an eligible domestic business, exemption applies by law unless waived. A waiver binds for **at least five calendar years**; returning afterwards requires revoking the waiver for a subsequent calendar year and meeting the eligibility conditions. Merely using the exemption does **not** impose that five-year commitment. It applies to eligible Gewerbe and liberal professions alike. **No Land or municipal variation in these thresholds or the binding period.** [§19 UStG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ustg_1980/__19.html)

**6. What varies by Land or city**

| Layer | Classification for this case |
|---|---|
| **Federal substantive law** | Tax classification; Gewerbeanmeldung obligation and commencement deadline; tax-questionnaire obligation and one-month deadline; VAT thresholds and waiver period; immigration authorization requirements; statutory chamber membership and relief framework. **No regional variation in those underlying rules.** |
| **Land rules** | Administrative responsibility and instructions; registration-fee legislation. Berlin/Hamburg fee ordinances and Cologne’s NRW tariff are verified examples. |
| **Municipal rules** | Gewerbesteuer **Hebesatz**, which the municipality sets within federal law. This is a real legal difference in tax payable. [§16 GewStG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/gewstg/__16.html) |
| **Local administration** | Office, appointment/submission channels, supported online cases, processing time and enforcement practice. These must be recorded separately from substantive law. |
| **Chamber/insurer rules** | IHK/HWK contribution schedules and accident-insurance tariffs. These are a separate category: neither ordinary municipal fees nor simply “Land law.” |

For software classification and residence authorization, differing outcomes can reflect different facts or application of federal law. **I have not verified a Land that legally makes all software developers Gewerbetreibende, or one that generally exempts them.**

REGISTERING A BUSINESS IN GERMANY

There are two questions, not one, and two authorities answer them separately. The tax office decides whether your income is from a liberal profession under section 18 of the income tax act or from a trade. The trade office decides, on its own, whether you must register a Gewerbe. The federal administrative court has held that the tax classification does not bind the trade authority, so you can be freiberuflich for the tax office and still have to register. "Freelancer" is not a legal category in German law.

If you write software for yourself, this is your problem. Engineering-like work can qualify as a liberal profession. What matters is both the work you actually do and the breadth and depth of your knowledge. A relevant degree helps and self-taught people can qualify, but they have to show comparable knowledge. Having a degree, programming professionally or calling yourself an IT consultant settles neither question. If you are unsure, put your actual services, your qualifications and some representative projects to both authorities. A tax number is not a ruling, and the binding ruling you can ask the tax office for under section 89 of the fiscal code does not settle the trade question.

Getting it wrong. If you should have registered a Gewerbe and did not, registration can be required retrospectively and a late or missing notification can be fined up to a thousand euros, and a tax reclassification can create trade tax for earlier years, though the trade income allowance of 24,500 euros means reclassification does not automatically mean tax to pay. If you registered something that was never a trade, the registration does not put you into commercial tax treatment. Ask the trade office to correct it and the tax office to classify you correctly. We could not verify a uniform official procedure for cancelling retrospectively or refunding fees and chamber contributions.

Registering the Gewerbe. You notify the trade office where the business is located, and the federal deadline is at the same time as you start the business. There is no nationwide grace period. Cologne describes warnings after four weeks and fine proceedings after six months, but that is how it enforces the deadline, not an extension of it.

The fee is not federal, and it is not municipal either. It comes from the Land. Berlin charges 26 euros, or 15 online, under its own administrative fee ordinance. Hamburg charges 25 under its economic administration fee ordinance. Cologne charges 26 under North Rhine-Westphalia's tariff, which the city collects. Whether you can do it online depends on the responsible authority, and sometimes on your legal form; Berlin lists forms for which there is no online route.

What registering sets off. Your notification is transmitted to the tax office, the chambers, the accident insurers, the employment agency, customs, the immigration authority and others. Transmission is not the same as somebody contacting you, and it does not complete your separate obligations.

Chamber membership is normally compulsory for an ordinary commercial business and there is no general opt-out, but the contribution can be nothing. If you are not in the commercial register and your trade income is no more than 5,200 euros you are exempt. A qualifying founder pays no basic contribution and no levy in the opening year and the year after, and no levy in years three and four, provided trade income is no more than 25,000 euros, and provided you had no business or self-employed income in the previous five years and no holding above ten per cent. These are profit tests, not turnover tests. One chamber's 2026 rates charge a non-register business with ten thousand euros of profit thirty-five euros a year, or nothing with founder relief.

What a foreigner needs. If you are a third country national, the self-employment has to be permitted before you do it. A permit tied to a particular job does not authorise a different economic activity unless the authority allows it, so check the title and its conditions rather than assuming a work permit covers it. You do not always need a separate self-employment permit: section 21(6) lets self-employment be authorised while you keep another residence purpose. We would not tell you that the trade office will refuse your registration without that authorisation, because we could not verify it.

The tax questionnaire, which nobody tells you about. Do not wait for the tax office to send you anything. Within one month of starting you must submit the Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung electronically through ELSTER. Registering the Gewerbe does not replace it. The tax office then writes to you with your tax number. We could not verify how long that takes.

The small business rule. If your relevant turnover was no more than 25,000 euros last year and is no more than 100,000 this year, you are exempt from charging VAT; in your first year the test is 25,000 of actual turnover. These are turnover, not profit. If you cross the current year ceiling, the transaction that crosses it is fully taxable, not just the part above the line. The exemption applies automatically and you can waive it, but a waiver binds you for at least five calendar years. Simply using the exemption commits you to nothing.

# Registering a business in Germany

**Agreed 2026-09-12**, after two turns in
`talk/germany/business-registration.md`. Eleven overclaims found at sign-off,
including a sentence of mine that was simply ambiguous: "if you write software

## What a reader is told

**There are two questions, not one, and two authorities answer them
separately.** The tax office decides whether your income is from a liberal
profession under **§18 EStG** or from a trade. The trade office decides, on its
own, whether you must register a **Gewerbe**. The Federal Administrative Court
has held that the tax classification **does not bind** the trade authority, so
you can be freiberuflich for the tax office and still have to register. Calling
yourself a "freelancer" does not determine which you are.

**If you work as a self-employed software developer, you need to establish which
classifications apply.** Engineering-like work can qualify as a liberal
profession. What matters is both the work you actually do and the breadth and
depth of your knowledge. A relevant degree helps and self-taught people can
qualify, but they have to show comparable knowledge. Having a degree, programming
professionally or calling yourself an IT consultant settles neither question. If
you are unsure, put your actual services, your qualifications and some
representative projects to **both** authorities. A tax number is not a ruling,
and the binding ruling you can ask the tax office for under **§89 AO** does not
settle the trade question.

**Getting it wrong.** If you should have registered a Gewerbe and did not,
registration can be required retrospectively and a late or missing notification
can be fined **up to €1,000** (§146 GewO); a tax reclassification can create
trade tax for earlier years, though the nationwide **€24,500 Gewerbeertrag
allowance for individuals and partnerships** means reclassification does not
automatically mean trade tax to pay. If you registered something that was never
a trade, the registration does not put you into commercial tax treatment. Ask
the trade office to correct it and the tax office to classify you correctly. We
could not verify a uniform official procedure for cancelling retrospectively or
refunding fees and chamber contributions.

**Registering the Gewerbe.** You notify the trade office where the business is
located, and the federal deadline is **at the same time as you start the
business** (§14 GewO). There is no nationwide grace period. Cologne's published
guidance says a warning fine may be imposed when commencement was more than four
weeks ago, and fine proceedings may be initiated after more than six months.
These are possible enforcement measures, not extensions of the deadline.

**There is no uniform federal registration fee.** In these three examples the
fees come from **Land** ordinances; the examples do not establish who fixes the
amount everywhere in Germany.

| City | Fee | Set by |
|---|---|---|
| Berlin | **€26**, or **€15 online** | Berlin's Land administrative-fee ordinance |
| Hamburg | **€25** | Hamburg's economic-administration fee ordinance |
| Cologne | **€26** | **NRW's** tariff, collected by the city |

Whether you can do it online depends on the responsible authority, and sometimes
on your legal form; Berlin lists forms for which there is no online route.

**What registering sets off.** Your notification is transmitted to the tax
office, the chambers, the accident insurers, the employment agency, customs, the
immigration authority and others. Transmission is not the same as somebody
contacting you, and it does not complete your separate obligations.

**Chamber membership** is normally compulsory for an ordinary commercial business
and there is no general opt-out, **but the contribution can be nothing.** If you
are not in the commercial register and your trade income is no more than
**€5,200** you are exempt. A qualifying founder pays no basic contribution and no
levy in the opening year and the year after, and no levy in years three and four,
provided trade income is no more than **€25,000**, and provided you had no
business or self-employed income in the previous five years and no holding above
ten per cent. **These are profit tests, not turnover tests.** One chamber's 2026
rates charge a non-register business with €10,000 of profit **€35** a year, or
nothing with founder relief.

**What a foreigner needs.** If you are a third-country national, the intended
self-employment **must be permitted before you exercise it** (§4a AufenthG). A
title tied to a particular job does not authorise a different economic activity
unless the competent authority permits it, so check the title and its conditions
rather than assuming a work permit covers it. You do not always need a separate
self-employment permit: **§21(6)** lets self-employment be authorised while you
keep another residence purpose. We would not tell you that the trade office will
refuse your registration without that authorisation, because we could not verify
it.

**The tax questionnaire, which nobody tells you about.** Do not wait for the tax
office to send you anything. Within **one month** of starting you must submit the
*Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung* electronically through ELSTER (§138 AO);
a hardship exception is available on application. Registering the Gewerbe does
not replace it. The tax office then communicates your **Steuernummer in
writing**. We could not verify how long that takes.

**The small business rule.** For a business established in Germany that has not
waived the exemption, qualifying domestic supplies are VAT-exempt where relevant
turnover was no more than **€25,000** in the previous calendar year and remains
no more than **€100,000** in the current year. In the year the business first
starts, the ceiling is **€25,000** of actual relevant turnover. These are
turnover, not profit, and the thresholds are nationwide.

**The transaction that crosses the applicable current-year ceiling loses the
exemption in full, not merely for the excess.** Ordinary VAT rules then apply,
including any other applicable exemption. The exemption applies automatically and
you can waive it, but a waiver binds you for **at least five calendar years**.
Simply using the exemption does not impose that five-year commitment.

## What this means for the schema

**A fourth distinction, and the sharpest one yet: who SETS a rule is not who
ADMINISTERS it.** Cologne's €26 is NRW's tariff, collected by the city. A model
that recorded "Cologne charges €26" would be storing the collector as though it
were the author, and would not know that every NRW city charges the same.

So a regional rule needs **the level it belongs to**, not merely the place a
reader happens to be standing. Together with Saxony's place-of-employment
trigger, SB-168 now has two reasons why a bare "region" column is not enough.

**And this case has two classifications for one activity**, decided by two
authorities who need not agree. Nothing in the schema models "two bodies
classify the same fact differently", and this is the first rule that needs it.
Filed rather than guessed at.

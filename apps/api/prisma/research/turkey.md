# Turkey: what a newcomer must do, and where the province changes the answer

SB-167. Researched through the GPT `search` conversation, the owner's order of
2026-09-12. **Nothing here is published to a reader until it has been turned
into rows with its source and its verified date**, and nothing was written from
memory: every line below carries the page it came from.

Session `01a09371-2c9c-7e33-a46c-8bcb6e01c583`. Read 2026-09-12.

## The headline, which decides how this is stored

For all six obligations asked about, **the law is national**. What differs by
province is administration: which office, how an appointment is allocated,
which documents a particular provincial office publishes, and in one case
which districts an application may use at all.

That distinction matters more than any single fact here, because it says the
region dimension (SB-168) is not "a different rule per province" for these six.
It is closer to "a national rule, plus what your province does about it". A
schema that made every rule provincial would invite fifty copies of one national
deadline, each free to rot separately.

## What the first pass established

### 1. Address registration (adres kayıt)

- **SETTLED, in its own conversation.** The agreed text is
  `agreed/turkey/address-registration.md`; the argument that got there is
  `talk/turkey/address-registration.md`. Four turns. The draft I put up for
  sign-off had seven overclaims in it, two of which I had written as caveats,
  and one sentence of invented colour. What follows here is the first pass's
  version, kept only to show what it looked like before.
  The first pass said flatly that a foreigner living in Turkey must report a
  change of address within **20 working days**. Checked back, that is too
  broad: the NVI instruction scopes the recorded-address process to registered
  categories, among them holders of a residence permit of at least 90 days,
  work permit holders and protection-status holders. It does not put a
  20-working-day duty on someone here on a visa or a visa exemption. Nothing
  is published until that case closes.
- **National:** the deadline, the offence and the process. The office is the
  local population directorate; a residence-permit holder may also notify the
  provincial migration office.
- **No provincial difference verified.** e-Devlet may cap daily slots by
  *district* and send cases needing review to the district office, which is
  capacity administration rather than a different rule.
- The late-notification fine is given as **TL 814**, set nationally, revised
  annually.
- Source: <https://nvi.gov.tr/sss-adres-hizmetleri>

### 2. Foreigner identity number and tax number

- The foreigner number starts with 99 and exists once the person is in the
  foreigner records; a potential tax number is obtained when a tax-linked
  transaction needs one, company formation included.
- **National**, with no provincial deadline, fee, document or appointment
  difference verified. Both are available centrally.
- **Explicitly not verified:** any official nationwide "obtain within X days of
  arrival" deadline. None is to be published.
- Sources:
  <https://www.nvi.gov.tr/kurumlar/nvi.gov.tr/mevzuat/nufusmevzuat/talimat/yabancilarakimliknumarasiverilmesiveadreskayitlarinintutulmasi.pdf>,
  <https://dijital.gib.gov.tr/foreigners/kimlikNoBasvuru>

### 3. Short-term residence permit

- Applied for through e-İkamet, then an appointment with documents proving the
  permit's purpose, the address and the statutory conditions.
- **National:** the online application, eligibility, the fee structure and the
  deadline framework.
- **Provincial administration varies**, and this is the one with real
  differences for a reader:
  - **Istanbul and Ankara:** the appointment date is set by the office and sent
    by SMS or e-mail; the applicant does not choose it.
  - **Istanbul:** first applications at addresses in **ten named districts**,
    among them Esenyurt, Fatih, Bağcılar and Zeytinburnu, have not been accepted
    since **20 October 2022**, with listed exceptions. A local restriction, not
    a statute.
  - **Antalya against Istanbul:** Antalya's live list requires, for a non-relative
    host, a notarised undertaking plus criminal-record and population-registration
    documents; Istanbul's published tourism list does not.
- **A reliability judgement worth keeping:** the Istanbul document list is dated
  **2018** and the Antalya list **2020**. The list e-İkamet generates for the
  specific application is what a reader should follow.
- Sources:
  <https://www.goc.gov.tr/kurumlar/goc.gov.tr/Yayinlar/Brosurler/2023/08IkametIzniBasvurusuNasilYapilir.pdf>,
  <https://www.goc.gov.tr/istanbul-ilindeki-yabancilarin-ikamet-izni-basvurulari-hakkinda>,
  <https://istanbul.goc.gov.tr/turizm-amacli-ikamet-izni-basvurularinda-istenilecek-evraklar>,
  <https://antalya.goc.gov.tr/ikamet-izni-basvurusunda-istenilen-evraklar>

### 4. Health insurance

- A valid policy is generally required for a residence permit unless an
  exemption applies. Separately, a foreigner resident for a continuous year may
  request SGK general health insurance if eligible.
- **National**, with no provincial difference found in either private-insurance
  acceptance or SGK enrolment.
- Sources: <https://www.goc.gov.tr/ikamet-izni-cesitleri>,
  <https://www.sgk.gov.tr/Content/Post/742c02df-68e1-422c-a387-fa2e4326b015/Genel-Saglik-Sigortasi-nedir-2023-01-25-11-25-46>

### 5. Forming a limited company

- Formed through MERSİS and registered with the trade registry for the
  company's registered centre. A foreign founder needs a tax number, and a work
  permit before working in the business.
- **National in law**, including the **30-day** registration deadline after
  signing. **The registry's mechanics are local:**
  - **Istanbul:** formation appointments only through MERSİS; the registry's own
    appointment site excludes them.
  - **Izmir:** an appointment after the MERSİS contract goes for approval, an
    SMS link, online payment at the signature stage, and a notarised Turkish
    passport translation where a foreign founder has no MERSİS record.
- Sources: <https://ticaret.gov.tr/ic-ticaret/sirketler/sirket-bilgiler>,
  <https://ticaret.gov.tr/ic-ticaret/ticaret-sicili/merkezi-sicil-kayit-sistemi-mersis>,
  <https://randevu.ito.org.tr/index.php>,
  <https://eportal.izto.org.tr/web/kurulus_randevu_bilgi.aspx>

### 6. Work permit

- Required before working, normally applied for by the employer electronically.
  An in-country application needs a residence permit with at least six months
  left; otherwise it starts at a Turkish mission abroad.
- **National throughout:** criteria, documents, channel, deadlines, decision
  maker and fees. No provincial difference. Sectoral rules differ by job, not by
  province.
- Sources: <https://www.csgb.gov.tr/uigm/calisma-izni/basvuru-turleri/>,
  <https://www.csgb.gov.tr/uigm/calisma-izni/belgeler/>,
  <https://www.csgb.gov.tr/uigm/calisma-izni/calisma-izni-degerlendirme-kriterleri/>,
  <https://www.csgb.gov.tr/uigm/genel-bilgi/harc-ve-degerli-k%C3%A2git-bedelinin-odenmesi/>

## What the second pass changed, and it changed two things I had assumed

### The order, and the dependency that was backwards

1. **An address and health COVERAGE come first**, because the permit
   application needs both: it must be filed before the visa or visa exemption
   runs out, the coverage must cover the requested permit period **on the
   application date**, and the card is posted to the address given.
   **Coverage, not a private policy.** An earlier version of this line said
   private insurance, which is wrong: it is one of four ways to satisfy the
   requirement, and some applicants are exempt outright. See the third pass.
2. **The first residence permit is applied for through e-İkamet**, giving that
   address. **A formal Address Registration System entry is NOT a prerequisite
   for a first application.** The Presidency states it is mandatory for an
   **extension**.
3. **Registering a changed address, within 20 working days of moving**, follows
   the permit rather than preceding the first application.
4. **The tax number waits for nothing**: it is obtained whenever a transaction
   needs it, before any permit or address registration. A foreign founder needs
   it before being entered in MERSİS.
5. **Company formation:** tax number, MERSİS registration of the person, then
   the company. Owning it is not working in it: a work permit is needed before
   the founder personally works.
6. **The work permit** in-country needs a residence permit with six months
   left; otherwise it starts at a Turkish mission abroad.
7. **SGK general health insurance is not a substitute** for the private policy
   at arrival: a non-student foreigner needs a continuous year of residence.

**This is the correction that mattered.** The assumption here was that a
newcomer registers an address first. A guide built on that would have sent
people to a population directorate in their first week for nothing.

### The 2026 figures, and what makes them stale

| what | 2026 | where |
|---|---|---|
| Late address notification | TL 814.00 | <https://www.nvi.gov.tr/adres-hizmetleri> |
| False address declaration | TL 17,051.00 | the same page |
| Residence permit card | TL 964.00, every nationality | <https://www.goc.gov.tr/belge-bedeli-ve-harc-miktari?v=1.0.8> |
| Short-term permit charge | **no single figure**: by nationality and duration | the same page |
| Work permit, up to a year | TL 12,574.90 + TL 964.00 card = TL 13,538.90 | <https://www.csgb.gov.tr/uigm/genel-bilgi/harc-ve-degerli-k%C3%A2git-bedelinin-odenmesi/> |

They are revalued by annual notice, **normally on 1 January**, on those three
pages. **That is not the only way they move:** the nationality-based permit
charge can change mid-year through reciprocity arrangements, so January is the
floor for re-checking, not the whole of it.

**The permit charge depending on nationality is a schema fact, not a
footnote.** `EligibilityCriterion` already carries a nationality dimension, so
a fee that differs by passport is storable; a single national number would have
been wrong for most readers.

### The ten Istanbul districts, and an honest gap

The notice of 19 October 2022 names exactly: **Avcılar, Bahçelievler, Bağcılar,
Başakşehir, Esenler, Esenyurt, Fatih, Küçükçekmece, Sultangazi, Zeytinburnu**.
First residence-permit applications at addresses there are not accepted from
20 October 2022, except for: family members of Turkish citizens and Blue Card
holders; family members of permit-holding foreigners registered in those
districts; certain property-based short-term applications meeting the
pre-restriction conditions, and their family members; in-patient treatment
applicants at the hospital address; investment-purpose applicants and their
family members; students at universities in those districts; and timely
change-of-purpose or extension applications by people already holding a permit
there. The Presidency reaffirmed the same ten in July 2023.

**Not verified: whether it is still in force in September 2026.** No official
notice was found lifting, widening or replacing it, and the 2022 page still
being online is not evidence that it is enforced. This is the one thing here
that cannot yet be published as a present-tense fact.

Sources: <https://www.goc.gov.tr/istanbul-ilindeki-yabancilarin-ikamet-izni-basvurulari-hakkinda>,
<https://www.goc.gov.tr/istanbulda-39-ilcenin-yabancilarin-ikamet-izinlerine-kapatildigi-iddialarina-iliskin-basin-aciklamasi>

### Where a permit holder reports an address change

**The official pages conflict and this is not settled.** The NVI FAQ uses
"veya", the population directorate **or** the provincial migration office,
which reads as a choice. The Presidency's general information page reads as
though a permit holder files with the migration directorate **and** the
population directorate. Those are two different guides for a reader, and
picking one would be inventing certainty. It is question 3 of the address
registration case.

What is not in doubt: the authority that issued the residence document must
pass the change to NVI within 20 days. That is the office's job, not the
reader's.

## What the third pass corrected, and where the fee table lives

### Who pays, and where the table lives

Exempt from the **permit charge** in 2026: Czechia, Denmark, Ireland, Kosovo,
Nepal, Sri Lanka, Syria, Turkmenistan, the TRNC and Palestine. **The TL 964
card fee is payable by everyone, exemption or not.**

The per-nationality table itself is at
<https://www.goc.gov.tr/kurumlar/goc.gov.tr/Kanunlar/u%CC%88lkelere_go%CC%88re_harc_miktarlari.png>
**and it is a PNG.** What is verified is that the official table found is an
image; that no machine-readable version exists anywhere is a universal
negative and was not checked. Either way, what this product stores has to be
transcribed by a person and re-checked rather than scraped. The
charge sits under the Law on Fees and is subject to reciprocity, so the list can
move during a year as well as each January.

### Istanbul, answered as far as it can honestly be answered

There is **no maintained official list of currently closed districts**. The
notices are historical: Esenyurt and Fatih from **15 January 2021**, the ten
districts from **20 October 2022**, and a central clarification of **16 July
2023** naming the same ten. The provincial announcements page exists but
carries no current closure list, e-İkamet is not shown to reject a closed
address at entry, and no current quota or saturation dataset is published.

So the guide says what is true and what is not known, rather than either
silence or a stale certainty: **these districts were closed by official notice,
last confirmed in July 2023, we cannot confirm the position today, confirm the
exact address and permit purpose through e-İkamet or the Istanbul directorate
before signing a lease.**

Source: <https://istanbul.goc.gov.tr/ikamet-izni-talepleri-hakkinda>,
<https://istanbul.goc.gov.tr/duyurular>

### Health insurance: round 1 was too strong

Private insurance is **not** required of every short-term applicant. The
coverage requirement is met by any one of: an SGK certificate under a bilateral
social-security agreement, an SGK provision certificate, an SGK general health
insurance application certificate, or a private policy. It must cover the
requested permit period.

And there are real **exemptions**: applicants **under 18** or **over 65**; a
medical-treatment applicant whose costs are all paid; a person whose
accommodation, subsistence or health costs are met by a Turkish public
institution; and up to **two companions** under Law No. 3359, Additional
Article 14. The age exemption covers short-term and student permits and does
not release a family-permit sponsor from insuring the family.

A bilateral agreement is not a blanket nationality exemption: the applicant
still has to get the provincial SGK confirmation.

Sources: <https://www.goc.gov.tr/ikamet-genel-bilgiler>,
<https://www.goc.gov.tr/ikamet-sss>,
<https://e-ikamet.goc.gov.tr/Ikamet/IstenenBelgeler>

## What went back into the conversation, and what is still open

1. **The trigger for the 20 working days.** From entry, from the lease, from
   moving in, or from the permit? And does it bind someone still on a visa?
2. **The order of the six, and the dependencies.** Can an address be registered

3. **The 2026 figures**: the fine, the permit and card fees, the work permit

4. **All ten Istanbul districts**, the listed exceptions, and whether the 2022

5. **One claim to check**: whether notifying the provincial migration office is
   an alternative, an addition, or the only route for a permit holder.

## The three holes a guide would still have

Named by the closing pass, in the order it would fill them, and each is its own
rule case rather than a footnote here:

1. **Entry basis and lawful stay.** Which nationalities need a visa, the
   90-days-in-180 rule where it applies, and the deadline for a first permit
   application. It decides whether a reader needs any of the later steps at all.
2. **What the e-İkamet application itself changes.** The application document,
   whether the stay stays lawful while waiting, the decision period of up to 90
   days, and the limited exit and re-entry rule during it. A reader whose visa
   expires while a decision is pending needs exactly this.
3. **What follows forming a company.** Tax office activation, social security
   registration as an employer, and any municipal workplace licence. Forming the
   company is not permission to start trading.

# Health cover in Turkey

**Agreed 2026-09-12**, after two turns in `talk/turkey/health-insurance.md`.
Every sentence below is the corrected one. It struck out "probably" from a
sentence of mine on the grounds that there was no evidence for it, which is the
standard this whole folder is held to.

## What a reader is told

**The policy your permit needs has a legal minimum.** The insurance regulator
sets it, in circular **2016/16 as amended by 2024/34**, in force since **1 April
2025**[^seddk-2024-34-in-force][^seddk-press-in-force]. Per year:

| Where you are treated | Outpatient | Your share | Inpatient | Your share |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| A provider your insurer has contracted with | ₺15,000[^seddk-2024-34-contracted] | 20%[^seddk-2024-34-contracted] | unlimited[^seddk-2024-34-contracted] | 0%[^seddk-2024-34-contracted] |
| Non-contracted, and public hospitals outside Annex 1 | ₺15,000[^seddk-2024-34-non-contracted] | 40%[^seddk-2024-34-non-contracted] | ₺150,000[^seddk-2024-34-non-contracted] | 20%[^seddk-2024-34-non-contracted] |
| The 20[^seddk-press-20-hospitals] public hospitals named in Annex 1 | ₺15,000[^seddk-2024-34-annex-1] | 20%[^seddk-2024-34-annex-1] | ₺250,000[^seddk-2024-34-annex-1] | 0%[^seddk-2024-34-annex-1] |

Treatment for sudden illness cannot be excluded, subject to the waiting-period
rules. We located the 2022/7 waiting-period amendment but could not read its
operative text, so we cannot promise immediate cover for every illness.

**Do not assume the policy you already own counts.** For an application made
inside Turkey, the policy must be **concluded in Turkey** with an insurer
authorised for the relevant branch, including an authorised Turkish
establishment of a foreign insurer. The circular allows a policy concluded with
an insurer abroad for a residence application made **abroad**, provided it
supplies the required minimum cover in Turkey; migration management says
residence applications **are not yet accepted abroad**. So do not assume the travel
policy you flew in with is the one your permit accepts.

**SGK, and the year.** A foreigner with **more than a year of continuous
residence under a residence permit**[^sgb5510-61-1c-after-a-year][^sgb5510-60-1d-foreign-residents][^sgk-gss-guide-continuous-year] may apply for foreign-resident general
health insurance, subject to the other eligibility conditions, including their
existing insurance status. Official guidance says residence under a residence
permit and does not restrict it to a particular permit type. We could not verify
exactly how the year is calculated or how many days abroad break it, so ask SGK
rather than trusting a number you read.

Joining is not automatic on your anniversary: for an eligible applicant, **GSS
registration starts the day after the request**[^sgb5510-61-1c-after-a-year][^sgk-gss-guide-day-after-request]. Routine treatment entitlement
normally also requires **thirty contribution days**[^sgb5510-67-1a-thirty-days][^sgk-yurtici-thirty-days] in the preceding year and,
for this category, no outstanding premium-related debt, subject to statutory
exceptions.

**What it costs, and it is not small.** The premium is **12% of twice the gross
minimum wage**[^sgb5510-81-1f-twelve-percent][^sgb5510-80-twice-floor][^sgk-gss-guide-twice-minimum-wage]. With the 2026 minimum wage of ₺33,030[^csgb-minimum-wage-2026] that is a base of ₺66,060[^tr-gss-base-2026]
and a premium of **₺7,927.20 a month**[^tr-gss-premium-2026]. Those last two figures were calculated
here from the official rate and the official wage; the same calculation also
appears in a copy of an SGK circular hosted by an accountants' chamber, which we
could not verify on SGK's own host.

**If you get a job, this changes.** Employment insurance starts on the day you
start work[^sgb5510-7-1a-start] (Law 5510, Art 7). Being insured from day one is not the same as
being entitled to routine treatment from day one: SGK normally wants thirty days[^sgk-yurtici-thirty-days]
of contributions in the preceding year, though it waives that when you move
between categories or from being a dependant[^sgk-yurtici-category-change][^sgb5510-67-dependant-waiver]. Somebody posted to Turkey under a
social security agreement may stay insured in their home country instead.

**Emergency care is not automatically free to an uninsured foreign visitor.**
Under the 2025 regulation covering visitors, emergency services and emergency
transport are **chargeable**. The regulation requires treatment to be provided
unconditionally and without delay, with collection afterwards; that is a legal
duty on the provider, not a guarantee of how a given hospital behaves. The
exception is a **traffic accident**, where SGK pays under the applicable
legislation and the patient cannot be charged personally. For a residence permit
holder without SGK, an official state-hospital pricing page says services are
charged under the public tariff; that page does not resolve every emergency
exemption for settled residents, and we did not verify them.

**By province.** The verified legal rules are national, with no provincial
variation. We could not verify whether office procedures or additional document
requests differ locally. For the private permit-policy minimum standard, Annex 1
public hospitals receive more favourable minimum coverage terms than non-listed
public hospitals, and provider network status affects the required coverage
terms.

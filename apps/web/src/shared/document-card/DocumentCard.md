# DocumentCard

Figma 18:65, 360x178. The name the reader will see on the form sits under
the one they know, and the requirements are text, because a misread icon
here costs a trip to an office.

## Props

- `localName`: Its name as the form and the office sign write it, "Öğrenci belgesi".
- `description`: What it is and where it comes from.
- `flags`: The requirements as words, never icons: "Original · No translation · No apostille".
- `status`: The status line where it says more than the state's word: "Missing, blocks step 4".

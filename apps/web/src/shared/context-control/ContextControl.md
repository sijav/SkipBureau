# ContextControl

Figma 44:542. No context is a dashed neutral invitation, not an amber error;
once the reader has said something it is a solid record of it; open, it
takes the accent ground. It states what SkipBureau knows, never a filter.

Partial, 44:527, says the gap inline: the nationality, then "· Add city" in
the quieter ink. Complete, 44:532, names the place in the same ink as the
nationality (SB-256).

## Props

- `known`: What SkipBureau knows, "From Iran". Nothing yet is the dashed invitation.
- `place`: Where the reader lives, once said, after a dot in the ink of what is known.
- `missing`: What is still to say, "Add city", after a dot in the quieter ink, where no place is said.
- `open`: Its panel is showing.
- `controls`: The panel it opens, for aria-controls.

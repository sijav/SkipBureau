# RoadmapStep

Figma 32:437, the spine of every process, 720 wide, 73 collapsed and 56 for
an upcoming step with no meta line. Current is the only filled ground, so it
is found at a glance while upcoming steps keep full-strength titles.

## Props

- `number`: Its place in the process, printed in two digits.
- `meta`: The line under the title: "About 2 days · ₺1,400 · notary". An upcoming step has none.
- `children`: What opens under the step. Without it the step does not expand.
